# 2026-11-16: a ship looks like what she is (row 355, branch `ship-art`, client only)

Owner: *"every pirate ship's graphic is same. the one with stronger ships, multiple fleets should have different graphics. this goes to every kinds of ship too"*.

## The cause

`src/chart/liveWorld.ts` drew a cargo, navy or pirate row as ONE fixed hull of her kind (`[PIRATE_HULL]`), whatever the read said she sailed. The read has always served what is needed: `hulls` (a class code a hull, flagship first, 0102) and `ships`, on `world.sea_roster` and `world.sea_traffic`; the class's `size` is on `world.snapshot().ship_classes`. No field was missing, so there is no migration.

## What is built

- **The tier rule** (`src/chart/shipShapes.ts` `shipTierOf`): served size 1 a sloop, 2 a brig, 3-4 a frigate, 5-6 a flagship. Nothing but a served size picks a tier. A class no snapshot has named keeps the kind's plain hull.
- **Four hulls a kind**, our own polygons: pirate (the forked stern), navy (the dart with a swept stern), cargo (the tub). Each tier is longer (pirate and navy 7 / 8 / 9.2 / 10.8 px, cargo 5.8 / 6.6 / 7.4 / 8.6) and has more to it (a beak, a gun deck, a stern gallery). Merchants and your own ships already had twelve silhouettes by rig and size, and keep them. Colours, deck marks, the red ground of a fight, your halo and selection are unchanged.
- **A fleet of several shows it at every zoom.** Close in, up to three hulls as before, now for the kinds too. The ships a mark does not draw are said astern: one dot, two dots, and from four ships a chevron. It is part of the flagship's own path, so it adds no element.
- **The battle stage** draws the same family large (`src/live/battle/ShipArt.tsx`): the chart's own outline for the family and tier, plus masts and sails (one, two, three), the navy's gun ports and ensign, the cargo ship's crates, the pirate's dark sails and black flag, your accent and pennant. The family is read from the served side (`sides[].kind`, `nation`, `mine`), the tier from the ship's served `t`.
- **A key** on the Codex's Ships face (`src/live/ShipsKey.tsx`), EN and KO.

## What the owner should know

As the world is built (0308, 0331, 0339), every pirate ship is ONE `xebec` (a brig here), every navy ship ONE `galion` (a frigate), every cargo ship ONE `navire` (a frigate). So on the live map each kind's ships will still look like one another: they ARE one another. They change the moment the world gives a kind a stronger class or a second hull, with no client change. Making pirates of several strengths is a server slice and is not started. The prod spread by kind was not read (the read was refused to the builder): the query is one statement over `pirate_ships`, `navy_ships`, `cargo_ships`, `npc_fleets` joined to `ships` and `ship_classes`.
