- **What works an item — one table, one generator entry per base type.** `scripts/build-gear.mjs`
  `USE_BY_BASE`: each of the 23 base types answers to MILES (canvas, rigging, lookouts), PIRATES (the
  High rack) or STORM (the Low rack and the Mid hull-keeping gear), or to nothing (the figurehead);
  an active's is always a day (PIRATES / STORM); guns consume a shot-and-ball and a gunpowder a fight.
  The generator's `use` (129 rows) is loaded into the side table `public.item_use` and held by the
  world guard (both directions, positive control: a bent trigger). **Why a side table** and not
  `item_profile.trigger/consumes` as the row says: an ALTER of `item_profile` holds ACCESS EXCLUSIVE on
  the table every ship read joins until the push commits. `consumes` is recorded only — nothing reads
  attack, armour or a trigger in a roll until SHIPS_AND_GEAR S4.
