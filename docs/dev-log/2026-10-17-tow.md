# 2026-10-17 — a wreck is towed to a shipyard, and a merchant's loop prefers one — `osn-0152-tow`

Migration **0152** (0149 skills K0, 0150 the stuck-laps fix, 0151 skills K1), `supabase/migrations/20260818000152_a_wreck_is_towed_to_a_shipyard.sql`.

**The problem.** 0142 left it to the owner: a fleet UNABLE_TO_SAIL (its flagship made port at durability
0) can never be refloated — `cmd.do_repair` refuses a fleet that is not DOCKED, and nothing else moves
it. Prod, read-only, 2026-10-17 (head 0146): 90 such fleets, all merchants, all lying in a port (none at
sea), 31 of them at a port that has a shipyard. 69 merchant loops have no shipyard on them anywhere.

**Decided by default (owner may reverse).**
1. A wreck can be TOWED to the nearest harbour with a shipyard, paying a fee per mile, and arrives
   DOCKED; then REPAIR works. One verb for everyone.
2. A merchant's loop prefers a shipyard: a loop with no shipyard is chosen only when no loop with one pays.

**What was built.**
- `public.tow_for(player, fleet)` — THE tow: nearest open harbour with a shipyard by sailed miles
  (`voyage.reach_from` + `public.port_has_shipyard`), 0 miles when it lies at one; fee
  `round(miles × tow_fee_per_nm × running_cost_mult)` through `public.pay_city` (`TOW`) to the city it is
  towed to; the `TOWED` event; its queue released (`cmd.clear_for`); DOCKED there. Refuses
  `E_NO_SUCH_FLEET`, `E_NOT_WRECKED`, `E_NOT_AT_PORT`, `E_NO_YARD`, `E_TOW_FUNDS` (new; have / need).
  No voyage is sailed — a tow is a service bought in port.
- `cmd.preview_tow(fleet)` (the real tow, rolled back) and `cmd.tow(fleet, version)` — registered,
  `authenticated` only.
- Merchants: `public.npc_tend_route` tows a wreck in port through `tow_for` and queues its REPAIR the way
  a player does (`cmd.parse` / `cmd.enqueue` / `cmd.advance`). Repaired at a shipyard its loop does not
  call at, its route pauses `off_route`; that branch now has `public.npc_plan` choose a loop FROM WHERE
  IT LIES and assigns it there.
- `public.npc_plan`: a loop with a shipyard that pays is tier 1, else 0; the best loop is the highest
  tier, then the best per real hour; a higher tier switches outright, within a tier
  `npc_route_switch_pct` as before. Off its loop, the present loop is tier -1 (never kept).
- Client: `src/live/TowNote.tsx` — the server's sentence and one `Tow · <fee>` button for each wreck of
  yours lying in the harbour, above PORT's faces and above the map city panel's tabs. History prints
  `TOWED`. `docs/WORDS.md` (wrecked, Tow), `docs/DESIGN.md` (§F.2 Tow, the two codes).

**The fee: `tow_fee_per_nm = 3`.** Prod's 90 wrecks: nearest shipyard median 48.8 miles, p90 672.8, max
993.8. Sailing under own power costs a median 0.15 a mile; the cheapest hull is 8,000, the median wreck's
hull 26,000, its full repair 1,100. At 3 a mile the median tow is 146 and the longest 2,981 — about twenty
times the cost of sailing it, and under 40% of the cheapest hull. 89 of 90 merchant purses pay the tow,
87 the tow and the repair (at 2: 89 / 87; at 5: 87 / 84).

**Proof (the migration's self-assert, on houses of its own, rolled back; dark and with the city purses lit).**
A player house wrecked at a yardless harbour: its REPAIR only waits; an empty purse is refused
`E_TOW_FUNDS`; the dry run quotes the nearest shipyard and moves nothing; `E_STALE`; the tow docks it there
(purse, city treasury, ledger, `TOWED`, queue released; the ledger reconciles); REPAIR then takes it; a
wreck lying at a shipyard comes in for 0. A merchant wrecked on a loop of two yardless harbours is towed
by the upkeep, repaired, pauses off_route, and is given a loop from the shipyard. The loop
choice's rule is three functions that read no market (`npc_loop_tier`, `npc_loop_beats`,
`npc_loop_switches`), proven on 15 cases; npc_plan is asserted to compose them. All nine hunks were
checked to occur exactly once in prod's live bodies (head 0151).

**The first deploy failed, and why.** Numbered 0150 for the push, the migration refused on prod: its
yard-preference probe searched 40 merchant homes for one where the live market made two loops pay, and
found none. A proof that waits for the world to fit is not one; the rule was lifted out of npc_plan into
the three functions above and proven on fixed cases. Nothing was applied (the push rolled back).

**Not done.** A wreck at sea (none on prod) is refused `E_NOT_AT_PORT`: the reach table measures from
harbours only. A merchant towed but too poor to repair lies docked at the yard until its company's
refounding (2 of 90 on prod at this fee).
