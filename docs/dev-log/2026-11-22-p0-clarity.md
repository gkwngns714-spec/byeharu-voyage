# 2026-11-22 - P0 (a): a new player can read the game (client only)

From the play-test in `docs/design/playable-2026-11-22.md` (rows 1, 3 and 10 of its table). No migration, no served read added.

## What changed

1. **First-run welcome** (`src/app/WelcomeCard.tsx`, mounted with the arrival and away cards in `AppShell`). One card on the Trade
   tab: "Welcome · You run a trading house in Lisbon, 1507. · Goal: earn your second ship. · First: pick a good, press Buy." and Start.
   - Decided by default: shown to a company whose served `fame.turnover` is 0 (the ledger turnover on completed buys and sells, so
     "has never traded"), on `/command` only, until Start. Start writes `bv:welcome-seen` in localStorage (wrapped in try/catch; blocked
     storage only means it may show again). The year is the top bar's clock (`gameToday`), the city the one her fleet lies at.
   - An overlay at the glass's foot (fixed, `glassBesideRailClass`), as the arrival card: nothing moves, nothing scrolls sideways.
   - `playwright.config.ts` gives every spec the mark (`storageState`), so no spec's fresh company meets the card;
     `tests/welcome.card.spec.ts` starts without it.
2. **The Trade cards' two prices have their words** (`src/live/TradeCard.tsx`): verified in the card, the first is `r.buy` (what the
   city asks: her Buy here), the second the city's bid `sell` (her Sell here), over the Buy and Sell buttons. Now "Buy 561 / Sell 518"
   (사기 / 팔기, the buttons' own words). The words stand where the prices' line is 9 rem or wider (a size container); in the
   narrowest cards (a 320-px phone, the 1,280-1,600 desk's four to a line, about 120 px) the figures stand alone, as before, so
   nothing is cut or scrolls sideways.
3. **The goal card** (`GoalCard.tsx`): the 0/4 is the build yard's "Ready 0 / 4", the lines of the cheapest hull's recipe stored at
   the yard (`useSecondShip`, `yardPlan`). It now reads "Second ship · materials 0 / 4" (두 번째 배 · 재료). Its press opens the step
   that is next: with the second ship's count, Office > City > Build yard (`openOfficeCity('building_yard')`, where Plan trip and
   Build stand); otherwise You, as before. The title line reads "Next: Mate" (다음:): bare, the next title read as a promotion she had
   not had (the coordinator's addition; both screens read `world.levels()`).
4. **Buy default** (`TradePanel.tsx` `firstBuy`), decided by default: the buy sheet opens at the smaller of the most she can take
   (the served bound: hold, purse, stock) and what HALF her coin buys at the price, at least 1. It opened at one lot, which at
   Lisbon was more than she could take, so everything: 70 cinnamon for 38,605 of 40,000 (before shot). Now 30 for 19,390.
   Applies to the docked Buy and to Sail & buy. **Not** to the trip a card's best sale opens (Buy & sell): that tray opens at the
   card's served units so its profit is the card's figure (`tests/command.bestSale.spec.ts`, 0351's one number). Halving that
   load needs the served best sale to price a half-purse load (a server change), or the card and the tray would disagree.
   **The profit stands above the fold:** a trip that sells carries "Profit +2,795" on the pinned money line beside the cost
   ("Cost 36,858 · Profit +2,795"), with the button; it was the fifth estimate row under the chart. One line, so the half-raised
   390x844 sheet still shows the units.
5. **Builder notes off player screens:**
   - You > Levels: the dark "ship and captain levels: counted only, no effect yet" note is gone (the lit one stays);
     the Combat tile and tray no longer say "Not counted yet" (`command.lvDark`, `command.lvCombatDark` removed).
   - Trade plan: "Sea rules off · all seas open" reads "Sea rules none" (해역 조건 없음); Codex/map seas: "Every sea is open."
   - Fleets > Room: a level rung reads "Trading level 1 / 3" (교역 레벨), a bought rung "Price 60,000" (가격); the bare numbers are gone.
