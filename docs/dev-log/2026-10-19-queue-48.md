# 2026-10-19 — the desk may queue a long passage: `order_queue_max` 12 → 48 (0193) — `osn-queue-48`

## Why

Since 0188 the desk plans a long passage as a chain of legs within the captain's Navigation reach (1,700 nm at
Navigation 0). Every hop is a SAIL line and some carry a PROVISION. A passage the owner wants to sail — Korea →
London, ~14,000 nm — is more than twelve lines, and `cmd.issue_lines` (0175) refuses a plan whole when it does not
fit the queue: `E_QUEUE_FULL`, nothing queued. The desk could plan the passage and could not queue it.

The cap is `world_config.order_queue_max`, 12 since 0001 ("DESIGN F.3: maximum orders in one fleet queue"). The
owner never set it. **Decided by default (coordinator, 2026-10-19): 48** — four times the old queue; room for a dozen
legs with a PROVISION between them and the trade lines at both ends. The owner may reverse it; the knob is one row.

## What 0193 does

One `update public.world_config set value = to_jsonb(48) where key = 'order_queue_max'`, with the row's description
saying why. **Guarded:** the file reads the row first and REFUSES unless it holds 12 — it raises the cap from 12 and
will not overwrite a value it did not expect.

Nothing else moves. Every reader composes `public.wc_int('order_queue_max')` at call time (wc_int is STABLE, no
cache), so no function is re-cut.

1. `wc_int('order_queue_max')` reads 48.
2. The probe fleet is put to sea on one `SAIL CAD` through `cmd.issue_lines` — the course is the two open-sea

4. A **30-line** plan queues whole — 30 rows, pending (the old cap refused this plan at its 13th line).
5. The queue is filled to **exactly 48** live orders; the 49th line is refused `E_QUEUE_FULL` with "the limit of 48".
6. The probe left no row; the grant wall, the read wall and the registry did not move.

The probe's company name had to fit `players_company_name_check` (3–24 chars) — the first draft was 25 and the
PGlite gate caught it; the second draft's SAIL was refused `E_NO_COURSE` (no path), the third `E_OFF_COURSE` (a course
starting at the pre-0076 Lisbon quay, 23.3 nm from her roadstead). Each was the chain's own guards working.
