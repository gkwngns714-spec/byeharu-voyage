# 2026-11-12 — S1: the yard plans the build (0358; owner rows 180, 305, 316)

Design: `docs/SECOND_SHIP.md`, step S1. Built 2026-11-12, finished 2026-11-13 after a reboot cut the first builder off.
**Not deployed.** Figures below are from the LOCAL world image (PGlite), not prod: prod's prices differ by live stock.

## Why
A new company could not finish a second ship. The yard named four sellers, but the two parts hide three more goods
(flax, hemp, iron); nothing said hull goods must be STORED while parts are MADE from the hold; no cost or weight was
added up; the Build button was hidden until everything was there; a full fleet refused only after the whole errand;
and the new ship came with no crew.

## What changed
- **Server, migration 0358:** `world.yard_sources(uuid, text)` re-cut by 3 hunks on its live body. Added keys only:
  - a good's row gains `qty` (the recipe's count), `bulk` (weight a unit), `ask` (`world.price(port, good).ask`);
  - a part's row gains `inputs: [{code, good, qty, bulk, port, name, miles, ask}]`, one row per `item_recipes` row;
  - the `sold` list now also sources the parts' goods and keeps the seller's port id.
  Still STABLE, SECURITY DEFINER, `authenticated` only. No table, knob, switch or job. No rule of play moves.
  News row: `Build: the whole list` · `건조: 전체 목록`.
- **Client (`src/live/PortYard.tsx`, `src/live/yardPlan.ts`, `dict/yard.ts`):**
  - one button, always there for a hull this yard builds: **Make parts** / **Store** / **Build** (off beside
    "Ready 2 / 6" while something is short) / **Hire crew · 8** after the build;
  - each line: need, in storage, on board; while short, the seller, miles, real time and cost;
  - a part unfolds into its goods, below itself; Total and Weight rows (Fits / Too heavy against the free hold);
  - **Fleet full** is said first, from `world.levels()` (`room.fleet_ship_max`);
  - a press moves nothing above it.
- **Finishing pass (2026-11-13):**
  - "Make parts" is offered only where the part is made in this city (the read says 0 miles). Before, a yard with no
    workshop would have offered it and `MAKE` would have refused every time, hiding Store behind it.
  - A seller row carries its figures on a second line. Beside the words, a seller's name broke inside itself at 320 px
    ("Buy at Berg / en").
  - Goods and sellers read in Korean through the one name map (`properName`): 목재, 리가.
  - The spec also runs at 320 px and asserts no word of the sheet is broken.
