# 2026-11-11 — 0351: a best sale she can pay for (`near-sale`, docs/SUCCESS_PLAN.md slice T5, owner row 314)

## The order
`world.best_sales(fleet, port)` (0348, re-cut by 0350) names, for every good, the sale with the biggest TOTAL profit at the
units her hold takes. For a new company at Lisbon with 40,000 coins that is Matsumae, Hagatna, Salvador: weeks at sea, hours
of real time; most cards cost more than her purse and open a tray that says "No money"; the card shows no time and no cost;
and the hop to Setubal that pays is never named. The slice: a best sale a new player can use.

## The rule, in two sentences
For every good the card names the sale with the **best profit per day among the sales her purse can buy** — where she lies,
at the units the docked tray's own Max allows (`public.fleet_buy_capacity`: her hold, the city's stock, the day's cap, her
purse), a sale counting when the buy (and her stores for the leg, unless they may be owed) is within her coins. The dearest
of those same sales is served beside it as the far choice, and a good with sales priced but none within her purse says
"No money".

## What 0351 does (`supabase/migrations/20260818000351_a_best_sale_she_can_pay_for.sql`)
`world.best_sales` re-cut, 3 hunks. Nothing else: no table, knob, switch, job; every other function is byte for byte what
it was (asserted), `world.good_board`, `world.board_sell`, `world.quote` and `public.fleet_buy_capacity` among them, so the
board and the tray's figures cannot move.

| Step, per good | What |
|---|---|
| The units | Bought where she lies: `public.fleet_buy_capacity(fleet, good)` is asked once. It is the authority behind the docked tray's Max (`world.buy_capacity` is its door). `bound_by` = hold: priced as before (FIT: the units that fit beside each leg's stores). Anything else binds (purse, stock, the day's cap): every sale is priced for that count. `world.board_sell` is asked exactly as `world.good_board(good, fleet, here, units)` asks it (basis `asked`, the buy quoted once by `world.quote`), so the card is still the board's row. A leg whose stores leave room for fewer than that count keeps its FIT figure. |
| Within her purse | A row is a candidate when she can sail to it, it is priced, it is not the city in hand, it loads no more than fits, and the buy plus her stores for the leg is no more than her coins. The stores are left out where they may be owed (`public.credit_on` at the city she takes them on): the trip tray's own "No money" test (`useTripGo`: `mustPay`). |
| The answer | The best profit per real second (the row's own `arrive_at` less now) among the candidates that gain; with none that gains, the least loss (`pays` false: "No gain", as before). |
| Served with it | `cost` (the buy), `seconds` (real seconds to that sale, from the row's own `arrive_at`: the server's days-to-real-time authority is the `time_compression` the row was made with; nothing new is invented), `short` (sales priced, none within her purse), `far` {port, units, profit, days, cost, seconds}: the candidate with the biggest total profit, when it is another city. |

No second cap rule: the file asserts the body names `public.fleet_buy_capacity(` exactly once and holds no walk (`while`), no
`trade_step_tuns`, no `wc_num(`.

A good bought at a city she does NOT lie at has no Max to ask (the cap authority answers for the city she lies at): the units
stay what her hold takes, and only the sales her purse buys are named. Her cargo (`on_board`) is not bought: only her stores
are asked of her purse.

The brief asked for "not slower". It cannot be had together with "use the tray's Max, write no second cap rule": the walk

own slice:** re-cut the walk inside `public.fleet_buy_capacity` to bisect the same sequence of counts (the same answer
wherever a dearer count costs more, which the price authority guarantees; about 7 quotes a good instead of up to 60). That
also speeds the docked tray's Max, which makes the same walk each time it opens.

## Decided by default (the owner or the lead may reverse each)
1. **The one cap authority over speed.** `public.fleet_buy_capacity` is called as it is; its purse walk is the whole
   slowdown. The alternative (an equal-answer search written inside `best_sales`) would have been a second cap rule.
2. **Profit per day is profit per real second to the sale**, with no port time, no return leg and no floor: a 10-second
   hop that gains 2,000 beats a 57-minute voyage that gains 35,000. The far choice is there for the other taste.
3. **The far choice is the dearest sale WITHIN HER PURSE**, at the same purse-capped units, not the hold-full figure the
   card named before (which she could not buy). For a company whose hold binds, it is the old answer to the coin.
4. **Anything but her hold binding the Max (purse, stock, the day's cap) prices the card at the Max**, not only the purse:
   the card never asks for more than the tray will sell her.
5. **Her stores count against her purse unless they may be owed** (`public.credit_on`), as the trip tray judges "No money".
6. **From a city she does not lie at, the units are not purse-capped** (no authority answers there); sales past her purse
   are dropped, and a good left with none says "No money". Locally 3 of Setubal's 4 goods read "No money" for a 40,000
   purse seen from Lisbon. Honest, and less useful than the home board.
7. **The card shows the time, not the cost.** Four figures do not fit a 140-px card; the cost is the tray's Total, one
   press away, and in the line's title.
8. **The far choice is a row in the tray, not a second line on the card** (the card is already two lines at 390 px).
9. **Her cargo's card** follows the same rule (best per day, far served) but the tray shows no far row for it: Cargo
   already lists where her cargo sells best.
10. **The branch carries a merge of main** (0350 is on main, not on `full-names`), so the PR's diff against main shows
    #589 and #590 too until they merge.
