# 2026-10-20 — distance pays a few times, not hundreds (migration 0212 (renumbered from 0213), `osn-haul-cap`)

**The problem (prod audit 2026-10-20, head 0211).** A far sale's distance premium (0173's haul) is paid by the city's people
(0174's IMPORT_DEMAND): money minted from nothing. The audit put it at ~57 % of every sale's value, ~500–600 M a real day
against ~1.6 B of world money, with routes ≥ 10,000 nm 30 % of merchant laps and 89 % of all profit. The far end paid
300.5 × the maker's price (haul_max 600 × share 0.5). The plan (WORK_QUEUE "PLAN 2026-10-20", build 1): cut the far end to
~4.5 × (haul_max 600 → 8) with 0208's refill pattern, sim first. Target (far-selling, approved by the owner 2026-10-19): a
long route ≈ 1.3–1.5 × a short route's profit PER DAY; laps ≤ 0 not much worse than today's ~33 %; no market emptied or
overflowing.

**Version.** 0212 (written as 0213; renumbered 2026-10-20 at the main session's order because insurance has no PR yet, so this deploys first on prod head 0211). NOT deployed: this PR does not push or merge.

## 5. The value chosen, and why: haul_max 3 (far end 2 ×), not the plan's 8 (4.5 ×)

Decided by default (the owner may reverse it in one line: the knob literal and `c_max` in the migration):
- The plan's own rule was "if 8 misses the target, pick the value that hits it". On the far-selling metric the owner approved
  the target on, 8 leaves a 10,000+ nm route at **6.0 ×** a short route's profit per day (5–10k at 2.7 ×); **3 puts it at
  1.45 ×**, inside 1.3–1.5. 4 puts 5–10k in the band (1.43) but 10k+ at 2.85. No value puts both long bands in the band.
- 3 is no worse than 8 for the merchants in the paired sim (§3) or for the starter (§4), and both are better than today.
- What 3 costs: distance as a price factor is small — London pays 1.89 × Mokpo for kimchi (today 196 ×; the owner's 0173
  order was ~500 ×, halved by 0208). The middle bands (2.5–10k) earn less per day than a short route at ANY value under 600
  (§2); the far-selling extras (plan item 7: saturation, stale far prices, spoilage) were meant to shape the far trade,
  and should be re-measured on this world before they are built.
- If the owner would rather keep the plan's 4.5 ×: set `to_jsonb(8)` and `c_max := 8`; its header table and §2 give its
  figures; its self-assert passed on the same world.
