## E2 — the figures watch the money

- **Counted** every 15 minutes: `public.stats_watch(p_now)` → `stats_world.watch` (new nullable jsonb). `tick_stats`
  gains ONE line after the world row's `get diagnostics v_w`: if the row was written, set its watch.
  Figures: `under_reserve`, `under_thin` (+ `thin`, knob `stats_thin_purse` 10,000), `broke` (purse < knob
  `merchant_broke_pct` 0.2 × capital — the §1 measure; E4 reads the same knob), `gifts` / `gifts_ducats` (24 h),
  `swing_p50` / `swing_p90` / `swing_steps` (|mid step| between consecutive recorded mids per pair over the hour,
  a fraction).
- **Served** by `world.stats_now()` under ONE new key, `watch` = `public.stats_watch_read(live total, now())`: the newest
  row's watch figures plus `new_slot` (its minted), `new_day` (Σ minted over the 24 h to it), `total_day_ago`,
  `day_ago_at`, `total_delta_day` (live total − the newest count at or before now − 24 h). Every old key unchanged.
- **Client:** `src/features/stats/Watch.tsx` (new), one line in `Overview.tsx` (after "New and moved money"), the
  type in `src/lib/rpc/types.ts` (`StatsNow.watch`, `StatsWatch`), the words in a NEW dictionary
  `src/lib/i18n/dict/watch.ts` (registered in `dict/index.ts`) so `dict/stats.ts` is not touched:
  Economy watch / 경제 감시 · New money / 신규 화폐 · Total, 24 h / 총액, 24시간 · Under reserve / 준비금 미달 ·
  Under {n} / {n} 미만 · Broke / 파산 · Gifts, 24 h / 지원, 24시간 · Price swing / 가격 변동.

Decided by default: "New money" in the watch is the last count's minted figure (the §7 trigger reads per slot);
the 24-h sum is already the Overview's "Printed, 24 h". Old rows keep `watch` null; the first count after deploy
(≤ 15 min) fills it, until then the watch shows new money and the 24-h move with "—" for the rest.
