## The fixes (0426), each decided by default (the owner may reverse it)

| # | what | old | new |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | `npc_min_return_pct`: a parcel is kept when it returns this % on its outlay after tax | 5 | **2** |
| H1 | a laid-up merchant whose harbour cannot pay moves her home (`public.npc_rehome`, one hunk in `public.waiting_ask_plan`, `npc_fleets.rehomed_at`) | (never moved) | **on a passage that pays** |
| | `npc_rehome_per_run` (new): moves a minute; 0 = off | - | 4 |
| | `npc_rehome_after_refused` (new) | - | 4 |
| | `npc_rehome_after_hours` (new): idle that long may move; moved, not again within it | - | 6 |
| | `npc_rehome_purse_min` (new): the new home's purse | - | 100,000 |
| | `npc_rehome_candidates` (new): harbours priced per ask | - | 3 |
| C3 | `convoy_load_max` | 250,000 | **100,000** |
| | `convoy_loads_per_run` | 20 | **30** |

**Decided by default:**

1. **2 %, not 0.** Still strictly profitable: a parcel must also net above nothing after tax, and the loop it rides must leave her more than
   nothing after wages, stores and every dock fee (0186, 0406); the purse bound (0410) stands.
2. **The move (H1).** It happens inside the waiting-asks job's plan step, right after THE planner has found nothing from her harbour pays, and
   only for a merchant refused 4 times in a row or idle 6 hours. Harbours offered: the legs she can sail now on her stores
   (`npc_feasible_legs` with her preset's days, `npc_legs_open`), open, no pirate home, purse >= 100,000, a good made there with fewer than
   `npc_buyers_per_good` merchant buyers; **her own country's first, then the nearest**; three are priced. Each is priced by THE leg planner
   both ways. **She moves only when the passage itself pays** (her harbour -> H: its parcels' profit after tax, less its wages, its stores and
   H's dock fee, above nothing) and her purse pays its stores. Not the whole round loop: that test is npc_plan's own, and the leg back into the
   poor harbour is exactly what fails it. The loop `[H, her harbour]` is written through the save door with her cursor on her harbour; her
   house's home becomes H (`npc_houses.home_port_id`: her origin, her home sea, her card). The open step resumes her under the quay like any
   paying ask: she buys here, sails to H, closes the lap at H, and at her lap start plans from H as every merchant does. If nothing pays at
   H she waits there; she has lost nothing.
3. **Left out:** pirate houses (0414/0418's roster rules stand: their homes are the pirate homes); a house with two or more ships (one home, one
   ship; 1 laid up now); a merchant moved within 6 hours (no hopping); more than 4 a minute (the ~45 eligible are each asked within 30 minutes,
   so all are tried within about half an hour).
4. **100,000 a load, 30 a run** (the measurement above; the brief's example). 50,000 would keep ~251 loaded but doubles the loads and their rows
   again for 82 more hulls; 100,000 is the step that stays well inside the budget. A raid takes 100,000 at most from one ship.

**Known edge, stated:** if the open step cannot take her at once (busy, a second's race) she stays laid up on `[H, her harbour]`; the next ask
re-lines that loop without a lap-start choice and resumes her, and if the market has moved so the round loop no longer pays, the planner
writes it with no BUY and she sails to H in ballast (0115's mid-lap rule, unchanged). The open step follows the plan step within about a
second in the same job, so this needs both a lock clash and a price move.
