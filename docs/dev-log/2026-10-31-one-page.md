# ONE PAGE — every tab fits the glass; secondary blocks fold; choices are one ordered grid (2026-10-31)

Owner rows 242 and 243 (quotes verbatim), branch `one-page`, **client-only** (no migration, no server
change, no text meaning changed).

## The law

On a phone (390×844) — and at the wide desk (1280×800) where the layout differs — **no main tab
scrolls the page**: `documentElement.scrollHeight ≤ clientHeight + 1` and the same for width, with
the main landmark on the glass. `tests/onepage.spec.ts` measures all ten routes at both sizes, plus
the choice grids and one fold. The City page is asserted and `src/features/city/**` untouched.

READ IT HONESTLY: the **document** obeyed the law before this slice too — the shell is
`h-[100dvh]` with `overflow-hidden` main, so the page could not scroll. What the owner felt was the
**tab's own Sheet** travelling under the pinned title: 39,410 px of catalogue on the Codex, 2,542 on
Stats, 2,442 on Command. This slice leaves the document law asserted for good and takes that
travelling surface away wherever the glass allows: the two worst offenders (Codex, Rank) now stand
still; Stats and Command are 26 % and 17 % shorter on the phone. The remaining Sheet scroll on those
two is the honest residue of content that cannot fit 755 px without deleting information (see
below).

## What changed, and the one authority for each

1. **`TileGrid` / `TileChoice`** (`src/components/ui/TileGrid.tsx`, `tileStyles.ts`, exported once):
   the owner's ordered grid. Equal cells on fixed column lines (`grid-cols-2…6` typed out because
   Tailwind cannot see a number), 44-px floor, accent wash + hairline for selected. `Segmented` now
   composes it for face bars; `FilterBar` renders its chips and toggles through it; the Money log,
   the great-powers picker, the port picker and the Trade city strip do the same. `ChipRow` — the
   ragged row this replaces — is deleted.
2. **`SectionFold`** (`src/components/ui/SectionFold.tsx`): one 44-px line with its key figure,
   closed by default, the block revealed directly under the control. Used for Stats' "New and moved
   money" and its mover panels, the merchants' best/worst day, best sales, and each Profit day. A
   press opens its own body; nothing above it moves; the information is never gone.
3. **`ScrollPane`** (`src/components/ui/ScrollPane.tsx`): the one bounded pane. The Codex catalogue,
   the Ledger feed, Rank's board, the Routes lanes, the Stats goods/cities/merchants lists, the
   Money log and the Trade card list scroll inside their own box while the composition around them
   stands. `data-scroll-pane` is the one attribute that says "this list may scroll".
4. **Density** where the design system allows: `SheetSection` 24 → 16 px of top air,
   `SectionColumns` 24 → 16, the Sheet's foot 32 → 24 px and its title bar 4 px shorter. No type
   size, colour, token or 44-px target changed.
5. **The City page was not touched** (`src/features/city/**` belongs to another slice); it is
   asserted in the new spec only.

## Decisions (decided by default; the owner may reverse)

- **"Foldable" is an example, not a spec** (the owner's latitude, row 242). Folds are used where a
  block is genuinely secondary AND long; the primary list on each tab stays open, bounded in a pane.
- **The pane is bounded by the glass, not by a fixed pixel height**: `calc(100dvh − N rem)` leaves
  the tab's own chrome (title, faces, search) room and collapses on a shorter window.
- **The grid is equal cells, not literal squares**: a literal square of a third of a 390-px glass is
  120 px tall, and eight of them would eat the page the owner asked to pack. "Square" reads as
  ordered and aligned; `onepage.spec.ts` asserts equality and alignment.
- **A row that labels a choice carries two lines at most**; long harbour names wrap rather than
  truncate (the align spec's CUT rule forbids hidden text without an affordance).

## What could not fit without removing information

- **Stats on a phone** still scrolls its own Sheet (1,866 px): the five-face tab carries the live
  RIGHT NOW band, World money (hero, holders, chart) and the live Money log with its 13 filters. The
  document does not scroll, the money log is a bounded pane, and the secondary blocks are folded;
  deleting any of those three would drop information the owner asked for.
- **Command on a phone** still scrolls its own Sheet (2,015 px): the Trade face's card list is now a
  364-px pane, but the cargo aid, the foldable mini map, the standing route, the queue, insured
  voyages and the ship card follow it. They are the face's remaining acts, not decoration.
