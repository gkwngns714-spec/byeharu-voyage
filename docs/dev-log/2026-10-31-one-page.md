# ONE PAGE — every tab and every face fits the glass; secondary blocks fold; choices are one ordered grid (2026-10-31)

Owner rows 242 and 243 (quotes verbatim), branch `one-page`, **client-only** (no migration, no server
change, no text meaning changed).

## The law

- **The document never scrolls** — `documentElement.scrollHeight ≤ clientHeight + 1` and the same
  for width, on every main tab at 390×844 and 1280×800.
- **The tab's own Sheet never scrolls** — this is the surface the owner actually feels. `Sheet`
  carries `data-sheet`; its `scrollHeight` must equal its `clientHeight` at rest, `scrollTop 0`, on
  every route **and every face inside a route** (the desk's four, Stats' five). Map and the City
  page are one glass and draw no Sheet.
- `tests/onepage.spec.ts` walks all ten routes, the four desk faces and the five Stats faces at
  both sizes, plus the choice grids and one fold opening.

## What changed, and the one authority for each

1. **`TileGrid` / `TileChoice`** (`src/components/ui/TileGrid.tsx`, `tileStyles.ts`, exported once):
   the owner's ordered grid — equal cells on fixed column lines (`grid-cols-2…6` typed out because
   Tailwind cannot see a number), 44-px floor, accent wash + hairline for selected. `Segmented`
   composes it for every face bar; `FilterBar` chips/toggles, the Money log's 13 filters, the ten
   great powers, the port picker and the Trade city strip use it. The ragged `ChipRow` is deleted.
2. **`SectionFold`** (`src/components/ui/SectionFold.tsx`): one 44-px line with its key figure,
   closed by default, the block revealed directly under the control on a press. Carries: Stats'
   World money, Money log, New and moved money, the economy watch, every money-flow period, the
   Markets filters and movers and goods list, the Cities list, the merchants' fleet charts / best
   and worst day / every company, the best sales and the captains' leaderboard; the desk's Cargo,
   Map (its city as the summary) and Orders (queue · insured · ship); each Profit day.
3. **`ScrollPane`** (`src/components/ui/ScrollPane.tsx`): the one bounded list (`data-scroll-pane`).
   The Codex catalogue, the Ledger feed, Rank's board, the Routes lanes, the Stats goods/cities/
   merchants lists, the Money log, the Trade cards, the City building face, the Invest holdings and
   town hall, and the Profit week scroll inside their own boxes. Never the Sheet.
4. **Density** where the design system allows: `SheetSection` 24 → 16 px of air, `SectionColumns`
   24 → 16, the sheet's foot 32 → 24 px and its title bar 4 px shorter; the phone desk's face strip
   8 px tighter and its Trade side 16 px tighter. No type size, colour, token or 44-px target
   changed.
5. **The City page was not touched** (`src/features/city/**` belongs to another slice); it is
   asserted in the new spec only.

## Decisions (decided by default; the owner may reverse)

- **"Foldable" is an example** (the owner's latitude, row 242). A secondary block with a figure
  worth printing stands closed; a list that is the face's subject stays open but bounded. Nothing
  is ever hidden without its 44-px summary line and figure.
- **The panes are bounded by the glass** (`calc(100dvh − N rem)`) or by a measured constant where
  the remaining composition is fixed (the Trade cards at 9 rem = one full card row; the City
  building / Invest / Profit bodies at 12–14 rem), so a shorter window shrinks them rather than
  the Sheet growing.
- **The grid is equal cells, not literal squares**: a literal square of a third of a 390-px glass
  is 120 px tall and would eat the page the owner asked to pack. "Square" is the ORDER; the spec
  asserts equality and alignment.
- **Every face is held to the law**, not only the route's landing face: switching a desk or Stats
  face cannot start the Sheet travelling.

## Nothing was removed

Every folded block keeps its line and key figure; every long list is still there and scrolls inside
its own pane. There is no route or face left where the Sheet scrolls at rest.
