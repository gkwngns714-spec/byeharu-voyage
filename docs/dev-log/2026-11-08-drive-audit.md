## 2. Defects, ranked by what the owner would notice first

1. **D1 — rows 241 / 242 / 248: the people do not walk.** The owner, three times: *"these dots are population, it can stop it can go back and forth, random movements"*, *"make the dot speed 3 times slower"*. `drive.hq` (eight runs, 1280 and 390, fresh loads): 0 of 36 walkers moved over 4 s (every dot's rect sampled inside the page; the first dot's x read every 100 ms held one value), the plan's two clips 3 s apart were byte-identical, the svg's SMIL clock ran, nothing was paused, the nodes were not remounted. The same walker markup alone on a blank page moves (x 100 → 121 in 3 s); in the app `svg.setCurrentTime(svg.getCurrentTime())` wakes them (9.6 px in the next 1.5 s); a clone of the svg appended to the body stands still as well. So the scene's SMIL timeline is never serviced until it is seeked — in Playwright's headless Chromium; **the owner can confirm at a glance in his Chrome, which this audit may not drive.** `tests/city.page.spec.ts` "the people walk" passes on main because it reads the dot right after a viewport change, when the plate re-scales under it. Code: `src/features/city/CityScene.tsx:983-1008` (`<animateMotion path dur begin repeatCount>` on circles without cx/cy). Evidence: `hq-hall-1280@2x.png` (a still picture, by construction), the run logs quoted in `drive.hq.spec.ts`; fixme "the people walk".
2. **D2 — row 247: the HQ's Investors list does not slide; it appears.** The owner: *"investors - tappable where when tapped, a list slides to the right where you can close"*. Sampled frame by frame from before the press: the drawer's first painted frame is already where it lands (left 856 at 1280, 0 at 390), `data-tray-mode="docked"`, computed `animation-name: none`. Cause: `src/features/city/CityHallPanel.tsx:176-190` opens a `Tray mode="docked"`, and `trayDockPageClass()` (`src/components/ui/screenLayout.ts:81-84`) carries no `bv-slide-in-right`; that keyframe belongs to `mode="side"` alone (`traySideEdgeClass`, line 104). Evidence: `hq-investors-1280@2x.png`, `hq-investors-390@2x.png`; fixme "the Investors list SLIDES in".
3. **D3 — rows 247 / 278: at a wide glass the Investors drawer stands 40 px inside the window's right edge** (x 856–1240 at 1280; the inset is the page margin, 80 px at 1440). The owner refused exactly this look on the map a day later (row 278: *"not totally fully on the right. i want it to slide from the right slide from the edge of the page"*), and #491/#493 fixed the map's tray alone (`traySideEdgeClass` → `right-0`). Cause: the same docked tray (D2), `lg:right-[max(var(--page-pad),calc(50%-40rem))]`. Evidence: `drive.hq` log "right 1240 (window 1280)"; fixme "the Investors drawer lands on the window's own right edge".
4. **D4 — row 302: the Production page's Cargo column contradicts itself on an empty hold**: "cargo ● 4 / 180 tons" over "Cargo empty." (the Fleets row says the same "cargo 4 / 180 tons"). The four tons are her supplies (15 days), counted by the bar and not by the list. Seen in every run. Cause: `src/live/PortProduction.tsx` composes the shared `cargo-bar`, whose used figure is the fleet's hold use (supplies included, `src/domain/fleet`), beside its own goods-only list. Evidence: `production-cargo-1280@2x.png`, `walk/w-city-production.png`; `drive.market.spec.ts` fixme "the cargo bar and the cargo list agree".
5. **D5 — young-world chart axes: "now · now · now" and a −1 🪙 floor.** On a world counted within one minute the Cities face's City purses chart labels all three x ticks "now" and draws a flat 0 line between y ticks "1 🪙" and "−1 🪙" (`walk/w-stats-cities.png`, `stats-cities-young-1280@2x.png`). Cause: `src/components/ui/seriesChartModel.ts` labels x ticks by "how long before the last point" and rounds under a minute to "now", and widens a zero-range y axis to ±1. On production the series span hours, so the owner would see it only on a brand-new city or world; low. Fixme in `drive.stats.spec.ts`.
6. **D6 — row 255: no loading percentage for the Map.** The owner: *"when reloading, for example opening the city, or loading the map, show percentage of its loading time"*. The meter exists for the City page only (`LoadMeter` is composed by `src/features/city/CityScreen.tsx`; the map's `map-loading` is a plain note). The city half was built and tested (`city.audit`); the map half never was. Low.

## 5. Spec notes

- Every drive spec skips when nothing is served (`reachable`), boots the shipped local image (never production), and runs at DPR 2 with explicit viewports (1280×800, 390×844, 1440×900).
- `drive.stats` counts a series the way production does — at the quarter-hour edges, with a credit between counts — and waits for a quarter with ≥ 4 minutes left before patching, so the tab's own mid-slot count (`localBackend.ts`, `STATS_SQL`) cannot add a partial row that production never writes.
- `drive.nav`'s seven-ship world wraps `public.earned_caps` to grant the room; `drive.hq`'s lit world lights the city treasury the way `stats.moneyFeed` does.
- Five `test.fixme` laws name D1, D2, D3, D4 and D5 so main stays green; D6 is a code read (no meter to drive).
