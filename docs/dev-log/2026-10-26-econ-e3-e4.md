## E4 — a capital gives (lit only)

| piece | what |
|---|---|
| `public.capital_pays(capital, nation, player, kind, amount, event, must, note)` | THE one way a capital pays a company. Pays from its surplus above reserve (`city_surplus`); the gap is CALLED from the realm harbour (`port_nation` = the country) with the largest surplus — `CROWN_CALL` −gap / +gap — then `city_pays`. Realm short → `{waiting}`, nothing moves; with `must` (a founding) the world's richest harbour pays it whole; if even that cannot, the old plain credit (stated; E7's guard judges it). `note` = the event to write once the payer is known (events are append-only, so `called_from` is in the payload from the start). Locks: the house, then the realm harbour, then the capital (city_pays's and city_day's orders). |
| `public.founding_credit(player, kind, delta, event)` | credit's drop-in for starting money: open = `public.credit(...)` exactly; closed = the company's country's capital pays (`capital_pays`, must). `new_house` and `npc_fix_hull_skills` call it (one hunk each). **`npc_found` is not re-cut**: its founding money passes through `new_house`'s `p_ducats`. |
| `public.capital_gift_pass(p_now, p_only)` | null while open. Lit: merchant companies whose **worth** (`npc_worth` = purse + cargo at cost, 0203's reader, restored — 0213 had dropped it) is under `merchant_broke_pct` (0.2) × capital, none in `gift_cooldown_hours` (24), under `gift_max_per_week` (3) in 7 days, at most `gift_max_per_hour` (50), gifted longest ago (or never) first, then poorest by worth/capital: her capital pays her capital — ledger `CAPITAL_GIFT` +, flow `CAPITAL_GIFT` −, a `capital_gifts` row, a `CAPITAL_GIFT` event `{port, port_name, ducats, worth, called_from}`. Realm short: one `GIFT_WAITING` event a day `{port, port_name, ducats, worth, gap}`. One sub-transaction per company (a failure is counted + noted). |
| `public.npc_tend_houses` ×3 | runs the pass when merchants are on; its `failed` / `notes` join the receipt; `gifted`, `gift_ducats`, `gift_waiting` are appended lit only (dark: the pass is null → `'{}'`). |
| `world.town_hall` ×1, `world.npc_fleet_card` ×1 | `|| public.town_hall_gifts(port)` (`gifts`: the newest 10 — name, ducats, at, called_from) and `|| public.npc_last_gift(player)` on the card's company (`last_gift`: port, name, ducats, at). **Each key is served only when a gift exists**, so dark (capital_gifts empty) both reads are byte for byte today's. |
| knobs | `gift_max_per_hour` 50, `gift_max_per_week` 3, `gift_cooldown_hours` 24, `town_hall_gifts_max` 10 |

Decided by default: "longest-broke first" (§5.2) is read as gifted-longest-ago (never first), then the lowest worth /
capital — the game keeps no "broke since". The capital pays from its surplus above its reserve (as CROWN_AID does), so a
gift never pushes a capital under its reserve; the call covers the rest.

**Stats, lit:** a capital-paid founding is a city flow `FOUNDING` (−) and a ledger `FOUNDING` (+). 0232's
`stats_window_kinds` sums them by kind → 0 new money. (On 0229's `tick_stats`, still live until 0232 deploys, the two would
be two rows of one key in a `jsonb_object_agg` — so E4 must not be LIT before 0232 is live. It is not lit until E7.)

**Client:** Town Hall "Gifts given" list (`Gave {name}` · `2 h ago` · ducats; KO `지원금`, `{name}에 지원`), shown only
when served; the day book adds `Gifts given` (`CAPITAL_GIFT` out) and counts `CROWN_CALL` in From / To the crown. The
merchant sheet adds `Gift 5,000 from Lisbon` (KO `지원금 5,000 · 리스본`) under her fortune. The Stats watch's gifts cell
now reads `count · ducats` (KO label `지원금, 24시간`).
