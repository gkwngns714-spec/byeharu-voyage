# 2026-10-25 — every ship sails three times faster (migration 0219, `osn-speed-x3`)

**The order.** The owner, 2026-10-20, verbatim (docs/OWNER_REQUESTS.md row 169, part a): *"I want the ship speed to
be faster for all ships including npcs. About 3 times faster."* Part b (smooth movement) shipped as PR #287. The queue
(row 169a) asked for this half to be sim'd paired, before and after, like 0208 and 0212, because it is an economy
change: every lap gets about three times shorter, so ducats per real hour rise about three-fold for players AND
merchants.

## 1. The one authority, and where the knob went

"How fast does she sail" has ONE formula in the chain, and this file does not re-cut it:

    voyage.ship_speed_at(ship, fill) = round(world.ship_stat(ship, 'speed') × M_hull × M_load × M_crew × wind_mult_v0, 4)   (0006 → 0074 → 0203)

`voyage.ship_speed` reads it with null; `voyage.fleet_speed_at` takes the slowest hull × formation × the captains'
speed % × the house's NAVIGATION; `voyage.fleet_speed` reads that at the fill she carries. Its base is
`world.ship_stats`' 'speed' row (0124): `ship_classes.speed_kn` (the RATED speed every card prints) + Σ flat item
deltas + flat perks + a line's flat set bonus, × skill %, floored at 0.5 kn.

Exactly as 0208 did for the hold (`hold_scale` 3), **0219 multiplies every flat speed quantity by one knob,
`speed_scale` (3), when it writes the rows**: `ship_classes.speed_kn` (103 classes, 3.80 → 11.40 … 7.10 → 21.30 kn),
`item_effects.delta` where stat = 'speed' (89 rows), `perks.per_rank` for the one flat speed perk, and
`gear_set_bonuses.flat` for speed (0 flat rows; the 2 pct rows are a per cent of a base that tripled and follow by
themselves). No body moves; no hunk. The rated speed on a card, the fitted speed, the speed she makes and every
duration agree, and a +0.6-kn suit of sails is still the same share of a hull. The data files keep the design
figures; `scripts/db/world-guard.mjs` now holds the rows = data × `speed_scale` beside × `hold_scale`.

**Why the rows and not the formula.** A multiplier inside `ship_speed_at` would have left the card's `speed_rated`
(c.speed_kn, 0074/0124's `ship_view`), the yard's stat lines (0133, 0163 read `c.speed_kn` as the base of a perk's
delta) and the fitted stat at the old figure while she sailed at three times it — three readers printing one number
and the sea honouring another. `wind_mult_v0` is reserved for wind (DESIGN K.1) and is not touched (asserted = 1).

with the base tripled the floor is three times harder to reach.

## 2. Decided by default (the owner may reverse each)

- **A voyage already at sea finishes her leg at the speed she left with.** `speed_profile` is frozen at departure by
  design (0006: "v_fleet per leg is FROZEN"); rewriting 800-odd profiles under moving hulls was not done. Every
  departure after the deploy is at ×3. The prod check below counts how many are at sea and the longest remaining eta.
- **Game time is unchanged.** `game_day_seconds` stays 2,880; a day at sea costs what it did (0210); there are a third
  as many of them per passage. The 15-second arrivals tick stays.
- `wind_mult_v0`, `hull_speed_floor`, `load_speed_penalty`, `crew_short_speed_mult`, the formation penalty: ratios,
  untouched. The 0.5-kn floor: untouched (above).
- The knob records what the rows are multiples of; changing it alone moves nothing (as with `hold_scale`).
