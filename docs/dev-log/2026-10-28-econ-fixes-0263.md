## The two fixes

| | was | now (design) |
|---|---|---|
| E5b (§3, "goods are never created or destroyed by the re-set") | a max that fell below the stock: the day's regen (`stock_after`'s `least(target, …)`) trimmed the stock to the max, destroying goods | under a multiple, a stock at or above the max **stays**; only the refill stops until sales bring it under. `public.depth_stock` is the one rule; no multiple (dark) = `stock_after` verbatim |
| E6b (§4b, "a city that can't pay shrinks the premium first") | a short purse shrank the whole lot, still at ×2 | a short city first drops the ×2 (the whole lot at the normal price); only if it still can't pay does the lot shrink (at the normal price), then refuse. Units at the normal price don't count against the want cap |

How E6b's fix works: one transaction-local switch `byeharu.want_off`, read by THE one wants reader `world.want_left`.
`cmd.do_sell` sets it only on the short-purse path of a wanted good, re-quotes, and clears it after the tally.

Hunks: `tick_market_drift` 1, `world.want_left` 1, `cmd.do_sell` 2 (each occurs once on prod at head 0259, checked
read-only 10-28).

## Decided by default (the owner may reverse)

- Under a multiple, no stock above the max is ever trimmed (a lowered max, or goods sold in above it). Harbours with no
  multiple (all of them while dark) keep 0010's rule.
- The whole premium drops, not part of it.
- A limit between the normal and the want price: after dropping the premium nothing is above the limit, so the sale is
  refused E_CITY_BROKE.

## Stats → Cities: a city's wants (client only)

`CityDetail` reads the city's market once on open (the store's `loadMarket`, the same `world.market` the quay reads)
and shows "Wanted ×2: …" / "수요 ×2: …". Absent while `city_wants` is dark.

## What each flag does once on

- `depth_by_investment`: each harbour's max stock moves once a game-day, at most 10 %, toward 0.5×–1.5× by its
  investment; stock above a lowered max is kept, never trimmed.
- `price_impact`: a buy pushes the price up, a sale pushes it down, inside the trade; the push fades by half a game-day.
- `city_wants`: every harbour wants 1–3 far goods and pays ×2 from its purse (10 % of its max a period); a short city
  pays the normal price first.
