**All 30 lies are stale.** `navy_hunt_hours` is 0.0625 (225 s) since 0423, but every one of the 30 has more than that left: 4 have 5-60
minutes, 6 have 1-3 hours, 20 have 3-6 hours; median 218 minutes, the longest 338. `public.navy_hunt_step` keeps a ship in port while
`hunt_until` is ahead, and each `hunt_until` was written under the old six-hour knob (at her station: now + 6 h; a far hunt: her arrival
+ 6 h). 0423 changed the knob and not the rows, and its own-sea patrol (its hunk 8) is reached only when a lie ends. Left alone the last of
them would sail about 01:40 UTC. Ruled out: unpaid, over the number, laid up, stations without legs (0423's read: 0 / 0 / 0 / 0, 168 legs);
the cadence (`tick_navy` every minute, `navy_steps_per_run` 40 for 43 ships, so every docked ship is stepped every minute); 0422's speed
(it shortens legs; it does not hold a ship in port); port time (0 of 43 in it). `navy_hunt_per_day` (0, the owner's) is the chance of a
meeting with a player, not a lie.

ships. Pacing is not the limit (the city run is hourly, but the need less aboard is under one load in every realm but Joseon, and
Joseon's is held by its donors' purses, not by the pace).

**Decided by default (the owner may reverse it):** cargo ships should be at sea whenever there is cargo to carry; with the cargo there is,
the one lever inside the rules is the size of a load. 0423 chose 1,000,000 a ship; the same need on 250,000 a ship goes out on four times
as many hulls, each sailing her whole circuit (hours), and a raid takes a quarter as much. Twenty loads a capital a run (was ten) so a realm
still sends its whole need in one run.

a run a home, money only through `public.convoy_book`).
