# 2026-11-14: the screens read as one design (the look pass)

Owner: *"and fix the graphics overall, especially map. it is weird"*. The map is another slice's; this one is every other screen.

## How it was judged
Every screen and tab was pictured at 390x844 (touch, 2x), English and Korean, in the shipped world and in the veteran's world
(eight fleets), plus 320x640 for the veteran: 260 pictures, opened and read, not measured. The faults, worst first:

| # | Fault (what the eye sees) | Cause | Fix, at the cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A goods card's Buy and Sell are bare words, on Trade and in the HQ Market | `Button` `secondary` is `surface-2`, and so is the tile under it | `Button` gains `tonal` (accent wash, accent word): the act on a tile. `TradeCard` uses it |
| 2 | In every tab bar the chosen tab is the darkest thing in the track: a hole, not a thumb | `Segmented`: track `surface-2`, chosen `surface` | Track is an inset (`bg/60` and an inset hairline ring), the chosen tab is raised (`surface-2`, `shadow-raise`). Colour only: no box moves |
| 3 | Stats opens every face with four one-line bordered boxes, about 280 px of frames | `StatStrip` is one column of `StatCard`s on a phone | Below `sm` the strip is ONE card and each stat a line closed by a hairline (about 210 px). From `sm` unchanged |
| 4 | Rows that open a page are two looks: flush in City hall, 12 px in with no visible reason in the Inn and the Port | `SummaryRow`'s fill was `surface`, the card's own tone | The fill is `surface-2`: a bar, as it already read on Trade. Two in a stack stand 8 px apart (`SUMMARY_STACK_CLASS`) |
| 5 | In the Fleets list the city is bigger and bolder than the fleet's name | name at the label size, the place at the body size | The name is the body size, semibold; the place is the label size, muted |
| 6 | On Trade the two doors (cargo, Map) touch the city chips under them | no margin under the doors' row | 8 px |
| 7 | Trade's title is 20 px; every other screen's is 24 | the phone desk's own header | 24 px (`text-t-figure`, the Sheet's phone title). The face still fits: `onepage` and `trade.flow` pass |

Also: `hover:bg-surface-1` (a token that does not exist) in `TradeCard` and `CargoBest` now names real tones.

## Left, and why
- **The map and everything under `src/features/map`**: another slice. `Segmented` is used once there (the city panel's tabs), so
  it wears the new colours; its geometry cannot move (a ring and a shadow, no border).
- **Codex goods rows wear the class wash unevenly**: the wash is the premium-class mark (0294, `good.class.spec`), an owner feature.
- **A flat price line in a goods card reads as a stray rule**: it is the day's line of a price that did not move; true data.
- **The HQ's building rail has five tiles in a three-column grid** (one empty cell): the words do not fit five across at 390.
- **A card's title stands 28 px over its first row and 12 px under**: every phone height in the specs is measured with it.
