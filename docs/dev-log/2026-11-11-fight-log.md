## What it is

**C1, the log (live at the deploy, display only).** A raid on a company's fleet keeps the engine's own numbers as one compact `log` (shape v 1): who saw whom, the raiders, each of her hulls, one row a round (shots, hits, damage both ways, hull left both ways, which ship was struck, the slip chance), how it ended, the boarding, her hull after. It is in the day (`voyage_events.payload.fight.log`) and in the VOYAGE_REPORT's fight entry, with what the day cost (`loss`). A ship of the world (merchant, cargo, navy, pirate) keeps none: her raid is byte for byte what it was. The English `rounds` stay in every payload, so an old bundle keeps working. One component, `FightLog`, draws it in History and on the arrival card; an event with no log draws its sentences as before.

**C2, the standing order (dark: `raid_orders_enabled` false).** Fight / Flee / Pay, per fleet, no row is Fight. Set through `cmd.raid_order`, frozen on the voyage's segments at departure beside `danger` and `raider`, read by the one engine `public.fight_sides` as the new options `a_order` and `rounds`. Absent, the engine's answer is byte for byte what it was.

- **Flee:** her guns are silent each round, she takes that round's fire, then rolls to slip on her own stream: 0.15 + 0.03 a point of sighting lead, never under 0.05 or over 0.30. Away is EVADED with `fled`. Never away in six rounds: boarded, as today.
- **Pay:** after the sizing-up and the first-sight slip, RANSOM before a shot: 8 % of the cargo at cost (`ransom_take`), from her purse to the named raider's home, or to the Brethren's capital for an anonymous raid. With nothing aboard, or nobody to take it, the Pay is refused before the fight and she fights; the log says why.
- **Fight:** today.

The client: the picker (`RaidOrder`) on the fleet's page and in the Send tray under the picked fleet, with the server's read beside it (the raiders' word, her slip as a word, what Pay costs, No guns). It draws nothing while orders are dark.
