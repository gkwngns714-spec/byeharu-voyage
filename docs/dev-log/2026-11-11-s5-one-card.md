# S5 — one goods card, and a short trade tray (2026-11-11)

The owner's remaster: "make it playable, make it fun", "much more simpler, easy to handle". Client only; no migration, knob or flag.

## What changed

1. **One goods card.** The card moved out of the Trade screen into `src/live/TradeCard.tsx` (a screen may not import another screen: tests/sections.spec.ts), and its pure row rules moved to `src/domain/market/tradeRows.ts` and `dayLine.ts`. The Trade board and the HQ Market (`src/features/city/MarketFace.tsx`) both draw it; the HQ's `QuayLedger`/`TradeRow` list is gone from the HQ. The card gained the good's class marks (in their own box beside the name, the height of the icon's line, so the card grows 0 px), the premium wash and the city's Wanted word — they were on `TradeRow` only — so the HQ lost nothing and the Trade screen's card wears the class too.
   **The best-sale line (0348) is omitted at the HQ, cleanly:** its press opens the Trade screen's trip to that city, and the HQ panel has no trip to open (and with no fleet here there is no read at all). The HQ does not ask `world.best_sales`; the line's place stays held and empty, so the card is the same height on both screens.
2. **A short trade tray** (`src/components/ui/TradeTray.tsx`, every caller). The body is the count, the button with the served total, and what an owner row wants in sight. Everything else sits under one **More / 더보기** row: Cargo space, "none aboard", Kind, Trend, Range, In stock, the bargain, the city's trades. More opens as a layer over the tray's body (absolutely placed over the body's own box), so the tray keeps its height, the page behind it does not re-flow and the button does not move. **Less / 접기** closes it; focus follows.

## Kept in sight because of an owner row

- Row 63: the Max row (the ceiling and what binds it) and the gauge with its chips.
- Row 74: "Bought at" on the sell face, and on the buy face when some is aboard; the Profit row beside the button.
- Rows 76 / 80: the Add-to-basket button beside the send button, and the Basket row on the HQ Market.
- The owner's 2026-09-14 sell order (count, Bought at, Sells at, You get with the share).
