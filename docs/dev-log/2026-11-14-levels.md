# 2026-11-14: levels are many, and each is raised by doing (0412)

**State: built, PR open, not deployed.** The design is `docs/LEVELS.md`.

The owner: *"levels we only have trading, exploration, combat. like rs3, i want diverse skills related to this game"*, and then
*"with exp table, exp info, with benefits when leveling up, what activity needs done etc."*

## What was there (read from prod, read-only, head 0407 then 0408)

- Three levels, all DERIVED: `public.player_progress` (0069, re-cut by 0127 and 0148) re-reads the ledger and the events each time.
  Nothing is stored. Seventeen functions ask it (titles, fleet room, captains' ceilings, sea gates).
- The curve: `level = floor(sqrt(points / 25)) + 1`, 1 to 20 (`level_from_points`, `level_points`, `level_line`).
- A level crossed pays Reputation 10 × the level and fires `LEVEL_UP` (0356), from a mark per track.
- Reputation (0352) already has one-line hooks in `cmd.do_sell`, `voyage.settle`, `cmd.do_build`, `cmd.run_fulfil`,
  `cmd.standing_route_close_lap`.
- What the 19 human companies have done so far (events by kind): 123 voyages by one company, 33 sales, 25 buys, 1 ship built,
  1 item made, no repair, no request, no share, no battle. Over those 123 voyages: 749 clear days, 17 hungry days, 2 storm days,
  1 calm day. A voyage is about 6 days at sea.

## What was decided by default

1. **The three old levels stay derived.** The order said "a single mechanism". They are in the same list (`level_skills`), the same
   page, the same curve and the same level-up moment, but their points still come from `player_progress`: moving them to stored
   experience would make a second authority beside a function seventeen others ask, on a live game. So the list has two kinds,
   `record` and `book`, and the page says which. A `record` level has no "today" and no list of grants: that is the cost.
2. **Ten levels now, three next.** Built: Trading, Exploration, Combat, Supplies, Weather, Shipbuilding, Requests, Craft, Routes,
   Investment. Next migration: Gunnery, Boarding, Raiding (the fight's end: 0408 to 0411 are re-cutting those bodies, and no
   company has fought yet). Dropped: Sailing, Cartography (Exploration already counts them), Diplomacy (Regard is its own score),
   Leadership (captains have their own levels), Banking (not lit).
3. **A level gives Reputation, and nothing new.** 10 × the level, the same gift the old three give. No new stat bonus: each would
   need its own knob, balance pass and a cut into a hot function. The page lists only what the server applies; for the old three
   it also lists the rules that already read them (fleets at Trading 3 and 6, titles and their seas, captains' ceilings, promotion
   levels), read from those rules' own tables.
4. **Names follow `docs/WORDS.md`:** Supplies (not Provisioning), Requests (not Contracts), Craft. "Seamanship" and "Shipwright"
   are Academy groups, so the storm level is Weather and the yard level is Shipbuilding.
5. **300 experience a day for each granted level**; a ship and a lap pay once; a lap only at a profit; days fed only on a voyage
   of 100 miles into a port with no hungry day. No back-pay.
6. **A request grants by its premium with no floor** (0352 found a floor a delivery pays a ton at a time).
7. **The word on the glass is "exp"** (the owner's own word), KO 경험치.
8. **Where it stands:** the Levels page (Command, Levels), in place of the three bars. Phone: a pressed tile opens a tray docked
   over the page. Desktop: the pressed level stands in the middle pane, above the ships and captains.

## What it costs (measured in the migration's own probe, on PGlite; the numbers are in its receipt)

| Path | Added |
|---|---|
| a sale, a buy | **nothing**: `cmd.do_sell` and `cmd.do_buy` are not cut (asserted) |
| a merchant's arrival | **nothing**: the hook is inside `voyage.settle`'s players-only block |
| a merchant's lap with no gain | one call that returns before any read |
| a merchant's lap at a gain, repair, share | one call that leaves after one `players` probe: **8 microseconds** |
| a human arrival | `public.level_arrival`: one read of her voyage's days and up to three grants: **0.48 ms** |
| `world.levels()` (the page's read) | 1.7 ms → about 5 ms for a probe human: ten levels with their rates and rules |

The beat runs merchants. What a merchant pays is the 8 microseconds, on the laps that close at a gain and on repairs and shares:
at 400 lap starts in ten minutes that is about 3 ms in ten minutes.

Proof 103 holds a merchant to nothing by count: after a direct grant, an arrival, 20 real laps closed at a gain and a real share,
`pg_stat_xact_user_tables` shows 0 tuples written to the four tables.
