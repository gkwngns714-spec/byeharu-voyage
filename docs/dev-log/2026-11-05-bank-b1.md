# 2026-11-05 - Bank B1: a bank in every capital (migration 0306, dark)

Slice B1 of `docs/BANK.md` (the spec; every default there is decided by default, the owner reverses afterwards).

## What it adds
- `banks` (23 rows, one per nation, `port_id` = the port of `nations.capital_port_code`, units 0, vault 0), `bank_accounts`, `bank_loans` (one OPEN loan per city), `bank_flows` (append-only through `public.forbid_mutation`, server-only).
- Trigger `banks_capital_only`: refuses any bank whose port is not its nation's capital (insert, or an update of either key).
- `public.bank_unit_price(nation)`: (vault + OPEN loans outstanding) / units, 1.0 when units = 0. STABLE, definer, server-only.
- 12 knobs (BANK.md section 6): `bank_enabled` false, `bank_seed_per_nation` 0, `bank_npc_deposit` false, the rest at their defaults.
- RLS: banks and bank_loans readable by signed-in players; bank_accounts only a player's own rows; bank_flows no client access.

Nothing reads these yet. No function, cron job, purse or existing knob moves (the self-assert compares every function body and every existing world_config row before and after).
