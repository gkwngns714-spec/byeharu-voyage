# The real straits are open (2026-11-16, migration 0446, owner row 352)

The owner: the fix over-corrected. Messina to Zakynthos is 630 nm where it was 291. The course goes round Sicily, not through the Strait of Messina, "a real, wide strait that ships must be able to pass".

0440 took every course off the land the chart draws. 0441 ended the approach allowance (a course could run over raster land within 25 nm of a harbour). Both were right. But both had been standing in for real straits that the 0.25-degree raster closes, and nothing held a strait open: the land guard only fails when a course is on the land.

### The known straits, each checked by name

A harbour on each side, the course the browser's course author plots between them, and whether it runs through the strait's own water (`scripts/straits.mjs`, 38 rows; printed by `tests/sea.straits.spec.ts`).

- **Open before and after:** Bonifacio, Gibraltar, Dover, the English Channel ports and the Solent, the Bosporus and the Dardanelles, Malacca, Singapore, Sunda, Bali and Lombok, Torres, Hormuz, the Bab-el-Mandeb, the Korea strait, the Taiwan strait, the Palk strait, the Sound, the Kiel bay, Magellan, Florida, the Yucatan channel, the Windward and Mona passages, the Irish Sea, the North Channel, the Minch, the Cook strait, the Bass strait, Tsugaru, the Corfu channels, the Kii channel.
- **Closed, and opened here:** Messina, the Great Belt, Bangka, Surabaya's western fairway.
- **Closed, and not opened here (decided by default):**
  - **The Kanmon strait.** Shimonoseki to Osaka is 562 nm, round Kyushu, against 217 straight. It was 561 before 0440 too: the raster never had it. It is narrower than the chart's pixel, so it would be a river carve (the chart's land excused in its cell), and it shortens every passage between the Sea of Japan and the Inland Sea. Its own slice, the owner's to order.
  - **Moon Sound.** The old Riga to Tallinn course (164 nm) did not use it: it ran across mainland Estonia, through raster cells the `baltic-gulfs` carve opens between its two gulfs (one record naming two waters, the fault 0079 mended in Tenasserim). Since 0440 the chart's land counts there, so the course goes round Saaremaa. The sound is shoal and narrower than the course author's grid.

### Not a strait: a long leg is measured as a great circle and drawn as a straight line

1,783 of the 2,500 pairs made no detour. A course's long leg (Callao to Fremantle is one) is drawn, sailed and judged as a straight line on the chart, and measured as the great circle between its ends (`docs/NAVIGATION_PLAN.md` section 7 records it). When the re-sailing turns such a leg aside at an islet, the leg becomes several, each measured on its own, and the same track measures longer: Banten to the Drake Passage 7,220 to 8,467 nm (17 %), Longyearbyen to Portobelo 4,899 to 5,514 at the Faroes. The pair next to it, whose line misses the islet, keeps the short figure.

This is not fixed here (decided by default). The two honest fixes both re-price every long haul: measure every leg along its drawn line, or draw and judge a leg along its great circle. Either is its own slice. Listed in `docs/NAVIGATION_PLAN.md` section 8.

## The fix, migration 0446

`20260818000446_the_real_straits_are_open.sql`, in two parts, each written by a generator.

### Part A (`scripts/build-straits.mjs`, template `scripts/straits.sql.mjs`)

1. **Four charted channels** in `scripts/sea-grid.mjs`, each polyline down the drawn water (the generator refuses one that touches drawn land). Six raster cells open.

   | channel | cells | what the raster had |
   |---|---|---|
   | `messina` | 2 | Sicily joined to Calabria |
   | `great-belt` | 1 | Zealand drawn out to 10.75 E across open water |
   | `bangka` | 2 | Sumatra drawn across the strait's southern half |
   | `surabaya` | 1 | no Madura; the fairway's cell land |

   Their drawn land stays land in the coast mask and in the audit, as 0440's charted channels.
2. **A fairway** (new, `fairway: true`: Messina and Surabaya). The chart draws Messina 2.6 nm wide off Capo Peloro and Surabaya's fairway 1.5 nm wide, in steps of the land bitmap that do not line up with the course author's 1.5-nm cells: charted alone, neither could be passed (measured: Messina to Zakynthos stayed at 621 nm). The fine cells a fairway's own line runs through are water in the mask (`scripts/build-coast-mask.mjs` `fairwaySubCells`). The line is drawn water at every 0.25 nm, so a course in a fairway is never more than one sub-cell (0.75 nm) over the drawn coast, under the audit's 2 nm.
3. **Messina's roadstead** moves from 38.375, 15.375 (the Tyrrhenian, 14 nm from the quay) to 38.185, 15.592: in the strait, 2 nm off the quay. It is `voyage.water_roadstead`'s own answer (0085: a quay in a channel's cell lies on that channel). No other roadstead moves.
4. **The zone of an opened cell** (step 1b). `public.sea_zone_cells` (0215) answered no zone for a cell a channel opened later: 0440's six cells had none, and Messina's roadstead now lies in such a cell, so the harbour had no sea of its own and `public.sea_leg_asks_keep()` failed on it. Every carved cell that is water with no zone takes the zone 0215's box rule gives its sea (12 cells: 0440's six and these six).
5. **The stored courses.** 1,670 distinct courses read (the chain's world and production's, exported read only at 2026-11-15 14:53 UTC, head 0442, gate green: beat mean 172 ms, 680 MB, 0 waiting on the disk). 38 are re-sailed, 46 with each one backwards:
   - every course to or from Messina (its roadstead moved);
   - every course that the course author's own search now takes through an opened strait more than 15 nm and 3 % shorter (21): a lane 0440 sent round Sicily or by the Sound goes back through the strait.

   On the chain's world: 25 of 1,141 lanes, 77 of 3,350 route stops. On prod at the export: 30 of 1,351 lanes, 70 of 2,538 route stops. Frozen cargo and patrol legs are re-cut (0440's step), and `public.sea_leg_asks_keep()` is called. Voyages already sailing finish their course.

## So that it cannot happen again

- **`scripts/straits.mjs`**: the list of real straits (38 rows), each with a harbour on each side, the box of water the course must run through, and how far over the straight line it may be.
- **`tests/sea.straits.spec.ts`** holds the built world to it: the course the browser plots runs through the strait, within its multiple, over no drawn land, accepted by the server's law. It also holds the owner's example with a ruler (the same world with Messina's two cells closed again: over 600 nm), and that a fairway is water down its own line and nowhere else. It runs on any PR that touches the pathfinder, the refiner, the mask, the sea grid, the ports or a migration that writes the raster, the roadsteads or the channels.
- **proof 148** holds what the database has: the four channels and their cells, each strait's own line accepted with every sample read and refused with a cell closed again, Messina's roadstead in the strait with a sea of its own, the five quotes within their multiple, every carved cell with a zone.
- **The migration's self-assert** holds the same on the server's law, with a rolled-back probe that closes each strait and sees its course refused.
- **The procedure** (`docs/NAVIGATION_PLAN.md` section 8): a change that lengthens a course is audited like one that puts it on the land. After any harbour, raster, coast or pathfinder change, compare the quoted distances before and after and look at every pair that grew by more than 15 % or 50 nm.

## Self-assert (properties, no totals that drift)

- 0440's table and 0442's function are in this world, or the file refuses.
- Each strait's own line is refused before its cells open and accepted after, every sample read.
- Every carved cell is water with a zone or land with none; every harbour's roadstead answers a sea.
- Messina's roadstead is what the rule and the table answer, and is raster water.
- Every new course passes `voyage.path_refusal`.
- Five named pairs (Messina and Naples to Zakynthos, Lubeck to Bergen, Banten to Lingga, Jakarta to Surabaya): the plotted course runs roadstead to roadstead, is accepted with every sample read, and is within its multiple of the straight line.
- A rolled-back probe: with a strait's cells closed again its named course is refused; a cargo leg and a navy patrol leg of two replaced lanes are re-cut.
- After the step: no lane or coast course holds a replaced course, and none is one the law refuses. Route stops are not asserted (a player may save one while it runs).
- Part B: 0442's own (the seed is the file, the three laws, a probe mended and rolled back; Messina's own row is its roadstead's sea).
