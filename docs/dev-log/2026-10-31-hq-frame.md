## What changed

- **One frame, two regions.** The frame is the header, the SIX building tiles (Hall · Market ·
  Storage · Forge · Port · Inn — one row at 390+, two rows of three on a 320 phone; every cell
  ≥ 44 px, names whole), the figures row
  (Population ≈, Gold, Status, Relationship, Your earnings packed into ONE full-width row) and the
  rail. A press changes only the split: the Town Hall keeps plan LEFT / panel RIGHT; an opened
  building takes the split's two regions as ONE panel at phone width, so the market's trade face is
  legible (366 px at 390) instead of 171 px wide, where it wrapped to a letter per line.
- **The hall fits without scrolling at 390×844** (content 503 px in a 503-px panel). The old panel
  was ~300 px tall because the five doors shared its column; the tiles moved to the frame.
- **At 320×568 the hall scrolls inside itself** — 336 px of served content in a 179-px panel (the
  tiles take two rows there) — and the page still does not scroll. This is the owner's allowed
  "measure and say what gives": the panel is height-bound by the 44-px tile rows, the 56-px rail
  and the figures row.
- **The City rail is the shell's own `NavBar`.** The hand-written City list (HQ · Map · Stats · Menu)
  is DELETED; `NavBar` derives every cell from the one tab table (`NAV_CELLS`), so the two rails
  cannot drift. Both read **HQ · Fleets · Map · Stats · Menu**; pressing Fleets from HQ lands on
  `/fleets` and HQ returns `/city`.
- The figures card is compact and full width; no row is spread over empty space. The population
  figure is `size="body"` and no longer clips (the `≈ 160,000` had been cut at the card edge).
- The plan marks the open place (one accent ring on its block; the port on the harbour front) and
  remains the whole, uncropped city (`xMidYMid meet`).
