# 2026-10-19 — a hold carries three times, goods cost a fifth, distance pays half (migration 0208, `osn-economy-x3-d5-h2`)

**The order.** The owner, 2026-10-19, verbatim: *"in addition to the 10x goods quantities, enlarge the ships cargo by 3x.
reduce the goods price by 5x. distance earning gold diminish by half"*.

**Version.** 0208 at writing. Prod head 0207 (every market ten times deeper, #263, live). It renumbers to the prod
head + 1 at push. Not deployed: this PR does not push or merge.

## 2. Decided by default (the owner may reverse each)

- **Only goods prices change.** Player and company cash, city purses, ship prices (yard ducats, build costs), crew
  wages, provisions (`provision_food/water_price`) and the profit-tax rates stay.
- **Repair costs stay.** `public.item_recipe_value` (read only by `unit_repair_cost`) now reckons a recipe at
  `base_value ÷ goods_price_level`, the design value. One hunk; asserted equal for all 225 items.
- **The goods a hull recipe consumes are bought at the new prices.** The yard's ducat price is unchanged, but the
  timber and the rest cost a fifth. Not compensated: they are goods.
- **The chart.** `price_history.mid` × 0.2 × (new haul ÷ old haul), per row, so the line meets the new level instead
  of dropping off a cliff at the deploy (63,356 rows on prod).
- **Stored per-unit prices × 0.2**, because they are prices in the old unit:
  - `ships.cargo_basis` and `player_storage.basis`. Without it every SELL "at profit" (every merchant's SELL lines;
    `npc_buy_budget` says "never below cost") would refuse the cargo loaded before the deploy for good. On prod:
    560 laden merchant hulls, 18.6 M of cargo at cost, and 3 laden player hulls (5,134 at cost); 966 basis entries.
    The cost: the profit tax on those first sales is reckoned on the scaled basis.
  - `standing_route_lines.price_limit` (2,809 on prod, all merchants' BUY ceilings) and an open order's limit
    (`orders.args.limit` and the "… AT n" of its text; 1 on prod).
- **Kept, as money already moved or promised:** open `trade_contracts` (123 on prod, 1.80 M of premium, 1.74 M
  escrowed from city purses; they lapse within `contract_deadline_days`), and the lap books.
- **Kept with the hold:** the warehouse (`warehouse_tuns_per_tier`, a flat 150, not a hold multiple), the contract
  sizes (10–30), `store_ratio` (stores stay a share of the hold, so FULL stores grow with it: a bigger hold has
  bigger casks, and an empty hull can carry three times the days of stores).
- **Affinity's own slope** (great-circle, at most 1.76×, 0005) is the market's geography, not 0173's distance
  earning, and is kept.

**What it means for the owner.** Merchants earn about a quarter of what they did per run, a third more of their laps
close at a loss or nothing, and about three times as many sit laid up. The prices fell five-fold and the distance
premium halved; the hold tripled, but wages, stores and repairs did not move, so the fixed costs eat most of a lap.
This is the owner's call; nothing was tuned to hide it. One 25-minute sample each; the two arms ran side by side on
one machine, so the timing rows carry noise (the kickoff and the sweep moved with it).

### A spec that measured the consequence: `tests/rpc.firstSession.spec.ts`

"Buy where it is cheap, sell where it is dear, come home richer" went red on this change, and the way it went red is
the balance finding in miniature. The spec searched the 24 nearest harbours for its own cargo, pricing one unit's ask
against one unit's bid at cities whose roster trades the good (haul 1 there), 20 units at a time:
- at 20 units the round trip's profit went whole to the crew's wages (`wages_from_profit`): she came home with
  exactly her stake (7,748 → 7,748);
- at 200 units (the day cap's floor is 210 since 0207) the best it found, cork to Porto, was quoted +4 and sold −3
  after the price walk;
- priced with the walk at the real quantity, none of Lisbon's reachable harbours paid (all of them tried).

The near-field gradient between makers no longer covers fixed costs. The spec now takes its outbound cargo from
`world.trade_routes` (the read proof 05 measures by; the spec's own search was a second answer to that question)
and keeps the claim unchanged: she comes home richer (wool cloth to SAI, 195 units, quoted +1,005; passes).
`CAP_SAFE` 20 → 200 moved with 0207's floor, as its comment asked.
