# 2026-11-16: Evasion, and a run that is her ship against theirs (migration 0453)

The owner, 2026-11-16: "number 5 needs a new skill, related to combat - evasion or something, like parley or speech or whatever. This skill
will increase your chance of running away along with speed level (or agility, you get the idea). Of course the success of evasion depends on
both myself and the other ships condition, it has to be relative."

Built as designed in `docs/design/evasion-skill.md` (written for 0452; 0452 is #752's escort refit, which touches only
`public.npc_ladder_refit`, so this is **0453** and deploys after #752).

## What was built

- **The level:** Evasion / 회피, the eleventh company level (`level_skills` order 11, kept by the book, 300 a day). `run_away` 40, `run_try` 5,
  granted once a battle in `public.raid_close`. Perk `run`: 10 % a level from 2 to 20.
- **The card:** `public.evasion_of` (new) gives each hull `ev` and `run`; `public.battle_hull_card` adds them, so the sheet freezes them at the open.
  `public.level_perk_at` (new) is the one sum of a perk's steps; `public.level_perk` reads into it.
- **The chase:** four hunks of `public.tick_fight`, behind `chase_enabled` (ships ON). Equal sides 15 % a roll, floor 1 %, cap 50 %,
  minus 5 points a failed roll. Off: 0450's chance to the digit.
- **Client:** the words (EN + KO), the tile's mark, `ev` / `run` on `BattleShipCard`, one more stat (Evasion) on a ship's card in a battle.
  The You page and Command → Levels draw the tile with no page code. The Run button and the log read the server's `rc` / `X`: no change.
