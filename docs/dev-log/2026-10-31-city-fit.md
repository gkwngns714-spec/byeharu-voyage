# 2026-10-31 — the whole closed city is always on the glass

Owner: *"i told you that i need to see all the city, a closed one."* (2026-10-31) — the second report
of the city being only a "section", after the closed walled city shipped in #417.

## The failure, measured

The plan panel (`city-info-image`) is `aspect-square w-full` inside a height-constrained flex column.
On a wide or short window the width wins the aspect ratio and the flex shrink flattens the panel into
a rectangle. The svg filled that rectangle and `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice"` scaled the
240×240 plate to COVER it, so the top and bottom of the walled city were cropped — the "section".

Before the fix, at 1280×900 the panel measured 699×677 and at 1440×900 788×677: the square plate was
drawn at 677 and the rest was cropped. The same negative free space crushed the four-figures card to
16 px (its padding only), so the `≈ 160,000` population hero was invisible on those windows.

## The fix

- `src/features/city/CityScene.tsx`: `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid meet"` (letterbox, never crop),
  and the svg's own background is the plate's ground (`bg-bg`), so the letterbox bands are invisible.
  `slice` is forbidden here: it is what cropped the owner's wide glass.
- `src/features/city/CityScreen.tsx`: the summary card's floor is its content (`min-h-fit` instead
  of `min-h-0`), so on a wide or short window the plan shrinks and keeps the whole city rather than
  the card collapsing.

The corners are the viewBox's four corners after `getScreenCTM()`; every one lies on the svg's own
rectangle, i.e. the whole plate is drawn at every shape. At the wide sizes the plate is
height-limited and centered, with side bands of the plate's own ground colour; the four edges of the
plate (wall on top/right/bottom; sea and coast on the left) are all visible, no cut wall or block.
