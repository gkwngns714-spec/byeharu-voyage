# 2026-11-12 — R4: a level crossed is a moment (0356, owner row 317)

Slice R4 of `docs/REPUTATION.md` (sections 1.5, 1.6, 5 and "R4" in 6): crossing a company level fires an event, pays a
Reputation gift, and is said on the arrival card, in History and on the Levels page. Built on the local PGlite world at
head 0353 (the cached prefix). **No production read or write was made, and the PROD CHECK files have not been run on prod.**
Branch `level-up`; no pull request was opened.

## What was built

**Migration `20260818000356_a_level_crossed_is_a_moment.sql`** (one DO block, `statement_timeout` 60 s, `lock_timeout` 5 s):

- Table `public.house_level_marks (player_id, track, level)`: the highest level of a track whose crossing was paid. Row level
  security on, no client privilege. A company with no row counts as 1. It is not a level: nothing reads a level from it.
- `public.level_marks_settle(player) returns jsonb`: a merchant (or null) returns null after one `players` probe. A human:
  one `public.player_progress` read; for each track (combat only while `playable`), for each level from the mark + 1 to the
  derived level: `reputation_credit(player, 'level', rep_level_per_level × L, 'level:<track>:<L>')`, one event
  `LEVEL_UP {track, level, reputation}`, then the mark rises. Returns `{levels: [{track, level, reputation}]}`.
- `cmd.level_up()`: the signed-in caller's own settle. `{ok, levels}` or `E_NO_HOUSE`. `authenticated` only.
- `public.reputation_lap(player, lap, net, route, lap number)`: a lap closed at a gain pays `rep_lap` (R1's knob, 10) once,
  key `lap:<lap id>`, inside the day's cap.
- Knob `rep_level_per_level` 10.
- Four hunks, sliced from the live bodies (`pg_get_functiondef` at head 0353, LF), each OLD text found once, each reversing,
  ACL / volatility / definer / config unmoved:

  | Body | The hunk |
  |---|---|
  | `public.reputation_arrival` (R1's own, 0352) | `perform public.level_marks_settle(p_player);` before its voyage read, after its merchant and switch returns. **No new hunk on `voyage.settle`.** |
  | `public.reputation_card` (0352) | serves `level_marks {trading, exploration, combat}` and `level_gift` after `seq` |
  | `cmd.standing_route_close_lap` (created 0092, last cut 0278) | one line after the `ROUTE_LAP` emit: `perform public.reputation_lap(sr.player_id, l.id, l.net, sr.name, l.lap_no);` |
  | `public.client_rpc_entry_points` | +1 row, `('cmd', 'level_up', '')`, **anchored on 0325's `('world', 'levels', '')` line**. 0354 cuts the `ship_moves` line and 0355 the `promote_captain` line; the `levels` line is on main and neither touches it. |

- No back-pay: the migration reads every human's levels (one `player_progress` each) and seeds her three marks at them.
- News row: `public.news_update('0356', 'Level up: Reputation gift', '레벨 업: 평판 선물')`, after the rolled-back probe; the guard
  refuses a world without `news_update`.

**Client**

- `src/app/ArrivalCard.tsx`: the level line reads `LEVEL_UP` events (it no longer compares two reads) and prints
  `Trading level 5 · +50 Reputation` / `교역 5레벨 · 평판 +50`. One line a track: the highest level, the gifts added. The
  events counted are those on the read that brings the arrival, those that land while the card is open, and those that came
  since the last card (a level crossed at the quay has no card of its own). All three tracks. The `+N Reputation` line now
  leaves out `level` rows, so the two lines never count one gift twice.
- `src/live/worldStore.ts`: the one `cmd.level_up()` call. After each house read, when a served level stands above its
  served mark, the shell asks once for that set of levels, then re-reads the ledger so the events reach the card.
- `src/features/command/levels/Levels.tsx`: each bar's line ends with the next level's gift, `Next +60` / `다음 +60`
  (`level_gift × (level + 1)`, from the house read; nothing at the top level or while the gift is 0).
- `src/features/ledger/headline.ts`: History words a `LEVEL_UP` row with the card's own sentence.
- `src/lib/rpc/{types,catalog,index}.ts`, `dict/feed.ts` (`levelUpRep`), `dict/command.ts` (`lvNextGift`), one row in `docs/WORDS.md`.

Things seen and left:

- A level crossed while the page is closed, or at the quay with no later arrival in that session, is said in History (and the
  Reputation figure moves) but on no card.
- `levels_bite` false at the deploy seeds every combat mark at 1. If the owner lights it later, each house's combat levels
  from fights already fought are then crossed at once and paid (10 × each level). To avoid that, read `level-before-seed.sql`
  (`combat_playable`) first; a follow-up could seed combat from the lit figure.
- The local world holds 13 houses that are not merchants (probe houses left by older migrations' own tests): the seed ran on
  them. On prod the seed is "every `players` row with `is_npc` false".
- `public.voyage.settle` calls `reputation_arrival` for every such house, so a non-merchant system house (if prod has one)
  pays one `player_progress` an arrival, as a human does.

## The deploy: what happens if prod is slow

- The seed reads come first and hold no lock: nothing is made before them, and the table's foreign key (the only lock on
  `players`) is the last statement that touches a table. A slow seed delays the file; it stops nobody.
- There is no bound of the file's own on the seed or the whole (0353's lesson: its own 40 s guard refused a deploy that was
  only slow). The one bound is `statement_timeout` 60 s: past it the statement is cancelled with nothing made, and a second
  push finds the pages warm. `level-before-seed.sql` makes the same 19 reads read-only: run it just before the deploy and
  the pages are warm already.
- The two measurements stop themselves after 2 s each and never gate. The real-lap part of the probe is skipped, and says
  so, where the world refuses the probe its route or cargo.
- A house that crosses a level between the seed read and the commit is seeded at the older level and is paid for that
  crossing on her next arrival: correct, it happened at the deploy.
