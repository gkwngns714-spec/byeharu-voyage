# 2026-11-15 pace-up (0421): the world is counted and read more often

The owner, 2026-11-15: **"like market price rewritting, make everything as much as more often, faster, as possible, get it?"** and, the same
day: **"there should be a room of course, not just too tight"**.

After the disk incident of 2026-11-14 several jobs were slowed or spaced (0409, 0412, 0413, 0416). The database now has headroom. This slice
spends some of it on what players SEE, and keeps the room he asked for: after this change, 0419 (the Stats count reads the ship tables), 0420
(every harbour repriced every 15 minutes) and the pirates' roster at full size, the database sits at about half of each limit in
`docs/DATABASE_BUDGET.md` or well under it. The one figure already over half before this slice, the size, is said plainly below.

## First thing to step back if the gate goes red

**The Stats count, back to twice an hour.** It is the biggest added load (+82 rows a minute, +50 s of work an hour, and the only change that
reads a lot: its `recent` and `day` phases), and it is the one that grows the database. The SQL the lead puts in a follow-up migration
(also the first part of `scripts/ops/harness/pace-up/rollback-0421.sql`):

```sql
do $r$
begin
  if exists (select 1 from pg_extension where extname = 'pg_cron') then
    -- budget: the Stats count (byeharu-voyage:stats) back to 2 runs an hour, about 2,450 rows written a run.
    execute $q$select cron.alter_job(j.jobid, schedule := '6,36 * * * *') from cron.job j
                where j.jobname = 'byeharu-voyage:stats' and j.schedule = '6,21,36,51 * * * *'$q$;
  end if;
end $r$;
```

Second: the client's read back to 30 s (`READS_PER_VOYAGE_DAY` 30 -> 12, a client-only PR). The two knobs are small; their old values are in
their descriptions.
