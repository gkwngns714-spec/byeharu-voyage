## What a player sees after the deploy

Nothing, until the owner lights the switch. Then, within the hour (`tick_city` at :37, then the cargo ships' legs): every city of a crown is treated as short under a reserve sized by its size, tier 2 = 200,000 d., tier 3 = 300,000 d., tier 5 = 500,000 d., a capital five times that (1.0 M / 1.5 M / 2.5 M d.), instead of the flat 50,000 d.; the realm's cargo ships load the difference at the rich cities and top the stops up to it, the capital among them with the largest target; a city under its reserve posts no request until it is filled; the hall's "funds on the way" figure grows with the target. Linear per tier, the game's own convention (stock = 200 x tier) | A tier-5 city's floor is 2.5 times a tier-2's where its payouts are 9 times: the floor binds the small cities that go broke, and barely the big ones (median tier-5 purse 1.3 M against a 500,000 floor) |

The size table is a crown's | Lit at today's purses: 133 cities and 7 capitals short, 36.7 M d. to move by ship over hours; 2 capitals are short today |
