# 2026-11-23 — 0478 the logbook: discovery and collections (dark: `logbook_enabled`)

Roadmap slice 9 (`docs/design/roadmap-2026-11-23.md`; content plan 5.3 item 6). RS3's collection log, Uncharted Waters' discoveries.

## What it does (lit)
- **One table of firsts**, `public.logbook` (player, kind, code; its primary key the guard): harbour, good, hull, captain, and the finished
  sets (region, kind). A row the first time only; at most ~1,200 a company in her life. Seas are counted from her harbours (all 51 seas
  have one). Merchants keep none.
- **Written at the two existing points**, never a scan of the companies: the arrival (`voyage.settle`'s VOYAGE_REPORT payload, one hunk →
  `public.logbook_arrival`) and the sale (`cmd.do_sell`'s SOLD payload, one hunk → `public.logbook_sale`). Dark both answer `'{}'` after one
  knob read, so the payloads are their pre-images'.
- **"Discovered"**: a first harbour adds `logbook.found` (region n / of, sea and whether it is new, up to three specialty goods) to the
  report; the arrival card draws it as a ring that pops, the harbour's name large, "Known for" with the goods' marks.
- **"New: Cinnamon · Spice 3 / 22"**: a first sale adds `logbook` to the SOLD event; the arrival card prints it under the sale.
- **Sets** (`public.logbook_sets_settle`, the one writer): every harbour of a region (25 sets) or every good of a kind (17). Once each:
  `logbook_set_rep` 100 Reputation (new reputation kind `logbook`, keyed, not day-capped) and a small lasting benefit in THE ONE dock fee
  (`public.port_fee`, one hunk → `public.logbook_fee_factor`): a finished region's harbours charge her 25 % less (`logbook_region_fee_cut`);
  each finished kind takes 2 % off every dock fee (`logbook_kind_fee_cut`). The card, the plans and the charge read the same figure.
- **The page** `/logbook` (`world.logbook()`, registered): five rings (harbours, goods, seas, hulls, captains) and the sets; then one
  collection at a time as set cards (ring, Done or what it pays) over a grid of slots: harbours by their country's flag, goods by their own
  mark, hulls by their picture, captains by a face; what she lacks is a silhouette. Its door is a card on You, drawn only while lit.

## Decided by default (the owner may reverse)
- Merchants keep no logbook. Seas are the seas of her harbours (no rows). Hulls and captains count what she has held at an arrival (and
  what she holds now); they have no sets. Goods sold means a quay sale (SOLD), not a delivered request.
- The lasting benefit is a dock-fee cut (one authority, the fee every reader shares), not a price or a cap (slice 11's goods mastery
  owns those).
- The specialty is the harbour's `port_specialties` goods, the most valuable first, at most three.
