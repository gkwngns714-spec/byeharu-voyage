# 2026-10-18 — proof 12's probe merchant laps on a stated market, not on the hour — `osn-proof12-clock` (test only)

**The red.** Main's `pglite-gate (merchants)` failed from `ef75f80` (0173, #195) on, and on every
main and PR run after it (39084523607, 39086615230, 39088373643, 39089347894):
`PROOF 12 FAILED: the probe merchant closed no profitable lap: net -9`. The other three legs were
green on the same runs. Reproduced locally on `origin/main` (11b8a9e) at 02:0x UTC, same message,
same −9.

## The cause, verified

Read off the failing run (a copy of proof 12 that stops after the switch and prints the state):

- The probe's route had **SELL lines only**, at both stops. Her lap-1 queue was
  `PROVISION DAYS 12, REPAIR, SAIL TO FNC`. No BUY. The lap closed at −9: her stores.
- She **was** planned before her lap started. The switch's pass (`npc_plan`) planned all 838
  merchant routes (`planned 838, ballast 145`), and `npc_tend` planned her again, alone, before
  assigning her. Both plans wrote her in ballast.
- Why: her ware was `anise`, the first Lisbon good by code. The leg planner (`npc_leg_lines`) keeps
  at most `npc_buyers_per_good` (3) **other** merchant buyers of a good at a port, and it counts every
  planned route, sailing or not. Three other routes already held `BUY anise` at Lisbon. Funchal,
  starved by the proof, is as visible to every Lisbon merchant as to her. The probe is planned
  **last**: the pass goes longest-planned first, and she was founded a moment before. So anise was
  struck off, nothing else on the leg passed, and she sailed in ballast.
- Her second lap can never start inside the proof. The fortnight at home (0101) is measured from
  `closed_at` = `now()`, and `now()` does not move in one transaction. So lap 1 is the only lap.
- Why it depends on the hour: the image's market is the wall clock's. The drift is redrawn by
  `random()` whenever a read lands in a later 10-minute slot than the image was built in. Stock
  regenerates by `world.game_day(now())` against the image's `last_regen_day`. Which goods the roster
  planned at Lisbon, and whether one place was left for the probe, changed with each run. On this
  file's pinned market (below), **all ten** Lisbon goods Funchal trades hold their three merchant
  buyers at Lisbon. Most of those routes never sail in the proof (`npc_fleet_max` 21 decides which
  start, not which are planned).

So the earlier diagnosis held on two points: the founding route plus ballast, and the rest. On the
third it was refined: she is planned before the lap starts, but the planner gives her nothing,
because of planning order and the buyer cap.

## The fix (`scripts/db/proofs/12_merchant_companies.sql` only)

1. **The market is pinned.** `proof.pin_market` (scripts/db/market-fixture.mjs) is called with this
   file's own key (`…0f12…dd`, `proof-12-fixture`). Every drift row is redrawn from the settled law,
   the drift slot is parked a day ahead of `now()`, and stock is set to `stock_target` at the current
   game-day. The receipt is read back: ≥ 14,000 non-zero, ≥ 1,000 distinct, no row at or behind the
   current slot, every `last_regen_day` today. Inside the one transaction the market is then a pure
   function of the chain.
2. **The roster is switched on first**, so every merchant line that will exist is written before the
   probe is chosen. A new non-vacuity check: the switch started ≥ 15 merchants.
3. **The probe's lap is built, not planned.**
   - Her good is the first Lisbon good by code that Funchal trades and that no **sailing** merchant
     buys anywhere, so no one fills the Funchal sink before her. It raises if there is none.
   - Lisbon is set to target stock and Funchal to 15 %, as before.
   - She is founded, and her lines are written through the planner's own save door,
     `cmd.standing_route_save_for`, as the planner writes them:
     - SELL at a profit at both stops;
     - at Lisbon, `BUY <good> <qty> AT public.npc_buy_ceiling(LIS, good, 0.30)`. The qty is inside the
       daily cap at both ends and half her hold.
   - She is started through the upkeep's assign door, `cmd.standing_route_assign_for`.
   - Asserted before the soak:
     - she is started, on lap 1;
     - her BUY is `done` through a route-born order;
     - she is SAILING.
4. **The assertion is unchanged.** "The probe merchant closed a lap with net > 0" still holds, as do the
   80 % roster lap rule, the route-born-order rule and all five other markers. Everything after her
   start is the real path: the executor, `tick_arrivals`, the SELL at Funchal and the lap close.

## The class, scanned (`scripts/db/proofs/01–11`)

- **11 (standing route).** It laps four times in one transaction. It owns its pacing
  (`standing_route_laps_per_game_day` 0, then 1, set in the file). Player routes have no rest (0101 is
  `is_npc` only). Its lines are written by hand, with no planner and no price limit, and it asserts
  only that goods were sold and the books balance. It does not rely on `now()` moving or on planning
  order.
- **04 and 05** already pin the market with their own keys.
- **02** drives `tick_market_drift` itself, on purpose (the soak), and asserts reconciliation only.
- **06** reads the market (spreads, its own fixed-key draws). **10** compares quotes for equality.
  **07** rewinds ETAs. **01, 03, 08 and 09** do not trade.
- None of them closes a lap or a day by waiting for `now()`, and none relies on planning order. Proof
  12 was the only instance.
