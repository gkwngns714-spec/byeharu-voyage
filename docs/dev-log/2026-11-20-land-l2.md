# 2026-11-20 · Land L2: the smith (migration 0461, proof 164) — DARK behind its own switch

Design `docs/design/land-2026-11.md` 8 (L2), 6.3 (Smithing's ladder), 5.8 (the market guard), 6.5 (a make at home). The land is LIT on
production (`land_enabled` true since 2026-11-19, the owner's word), so L2 ships behind **its own switch, `land_smith_enabled`
(false)**. `land_enabled` and `land_yield_pct` are not touched.

## What the switch hides (while `land_smith_enabled` is false)

- **Nothing a player sees moves.** The 0461 self-assert reads, for one Lisbon company of Mining with the land lit, every Mining job's
  terms (`public.land_terms`), the Land read (`world.land('mining')`) and the Levels card BEFORE its hunks and after them, and they are
  the same text (the Land read less three new keys the shipped client does not read: `skills` = `["mining"]`, each job's `kind` and
  `make`).
- Hidden: the **Smithing tile** on You's Levels (the card serves a land skill only while `public.land_skill_on`), the **Smithing face** on
  the Land tab (the tab draws its faces from `skills`), every **smith's Send** (refused `E_NOT_IN_USE` before it writes), and
  `world.land('smithing')` (answers as for no skill). The Forge is exactly the Forge (its take moved into `public.forge_take`; proof 164
  makes 6 Iron Bars at the same cost and reads the same short sentence).
- Lighting it is the owner's: `update public.world_config set value = 'true'::jsonb where key = 'land_smith_enabled'`. False again is the
  rollback (a smith out keeps its row; its Collect still books).

## Decided by default (the owner may reverse any)

1. **Cut at a clean seam: Smithing's 21 recipes and the frame's make kind ship; the made picks do not.** An Iron (6 %), Steel (12 %) or
   Wootz (18 %) pick is faster than the plain pick (3 %), which 0457's guard assumes is the best tool (`FASTEST.toolPct`): Mining's
   tightest job-regions are at 0.9999, so any better pick breaks the guard unless Mining's 57 rows are re-timed slower for everyone by
   (1 - 0.03) / (1 - the pick's speed), which changes live play on a lit land. That is the owner's: re-time Mining for a best pick of
   X %, or leave tools at 3 %.
2. **The Forge keeps every recipe** (design 5.9 / 10 no. 14 would make it refuse a recipe Smithing owns): it is his Production page.
   Today the Forge makes Iron Bars at once with a docked fleet, and the smith takes 6 h 56 min: the smith is for a company with no ship
   in port, and for Smithing's level.
3. **Fresh Water is not free at home** (design L2's `land_free_goods`): since 0460 Bilbao's quay sells it; a free input is goods from
   nothing. Sword Blades, Talwar, Katana and Toledo Blades need it bought.
4. **A smith's Send makes as many as the home Storage holds the inputs for** (`make.can`, the scarcest input), and takes them all at
   once; Call home stops it and gives back the inputs of the units not made (at the cost they were taken at). No count is asked: one
   Send door for every job.
5. **The guard's target for a made good is the least middling target over all 25 regions** (a home can be in any), the strictest
   reading, as 0457 took for Mining.
6. **`land_yield_pct` is one dial over make jobs too** (one formula, `public.land_terms`): at 50 a bar takes twice as long.
7. **A made unit is booked at the Forge's cost**: its inputs' blended cost per tun (0081's one weighted average, the Forge's own
   arithmetic). A made unit that waits for room (Storage full) is booked at 0 when it fits, as a dug one is; inputs given back that do
   not fit wait with the party in `found_waiting`, as a find does, also at 0.
8. **No second unit for company on a make job**, and a smith at home is nobody's company: company counts parties on the same ground.
9. **No tool speeds a make job** (no hammer: the design names one "later").
10. **Rows from level 22 that need Planks, Leather or Woollen Cloth ship as rows** (the design opens them "when those skills ship"):
    they stay short with the server's E_NO_MATERIALS until those goods are in Storage (the Forge makes Planks; a standing order can bring
    any).
11. **The standing order from a recipe's row** (design L2) is not built: the tray names the quays that sell the product, not the inputs.
