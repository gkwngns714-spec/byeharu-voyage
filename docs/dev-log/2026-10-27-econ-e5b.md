| `tick_market_drift` (3 hunks) | 0247's once-a-harbour CTE also reads `depth_mult`; the day's regen writes the new max in the row write it already makes. No new statement, no new row write. |

## Decided by default (the owner may reverse)

- "Once a day" = once per **game-day**, in `tick_city`, as E5's `restock_day` does. One rule for both settings.
- "±10 % of depth" = the multiple moves at most **10 % of itself** a day: 1.0 → 1.1 → 1.21 …; 1.0 → 0.9 → 0.81 …
- Lit, every harbour starts at **1.0×** (today's max) and walks toward the formula. No jump.
- Switched off after being lit: each harbour walks back to 1.0× by the same step, then stays at 1.0× (= the base).
- The stock is **not rescaled**; it walks to the new max at the day's restock rate. When the max falls under the stock,
  the existing regen rule (`stock_after`'s `least(target, …)`, since 0010) trims the stock to the max at that day's
  regen, exactly as it already trims goods sold in above the max. The 10 %/day step bounds how much.
- Its own flag, not E5's `restock_by_investment`: the two can be lit separately.
- The outlook (`price_path`) keeps the max as it stands now.
