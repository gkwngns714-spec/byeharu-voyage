## What is built

- **The level system, one system with two curves as data.** `public.level_curves` (the long curve, 100 rows:
  `100 x (1.1^(L-1) - 1)` rounded down, level 100 at 1,252,682). `public.level_skills` gains `side`, `curve`, `gift_every`; a land
  skill may have no limit a day. `public.level_of / level_begins / level_top / level_line_of` are THE reading of a skill's level; a
  short skill's goes straight to `level_from_points / level_points / level_line`, untouched. Three hunks: `level_grant` (the level on
  the skill's curve; a land skill pays its Reputation gift every 5th level, `10 x L / 5`), `level_perk`, `level_skills_card` (each
  skill's own line, top, side, curve and gift; a land skill only while lit). The self-assert proves every sea skill's level is what it
  was for every experience 0 to 9,100.
- **Mining**: one `level_skills` row (land, long, gift every 5), one activity (`dig`), 57 jobs in `public.land_actions` generated by
  `scripts/build-land-migration.mjs` (6.3's ladder + the rule: an hour of a job worth 4,800 at level 1 to 15,000 at 100 at base
  value, never under 40 s), held to the generator by `tests/land.data.spec.ts`.
- **The land**: `public.land_sites` (the mines of the 25 regions, a 15-s march), `public.land_tools` (the plain pick, 3 %, 300 coin),
  `public.land_parties` (one row a company).
- **Readings that write nothing**: `land_passage(port, good)` (home, or the nearest harbour of a region the good comes from by
  `voyage.reach_from`, at `land_passage_seconds_per_nm`); `land_terms(player, action)` (what a Send would do, or the refusal it would
  meet); `land_progress(party, at)` (the sum of 6.5, cap and all).
- **The one writer**: `land_settle(party, at)` books goods through `public.storage_put` at a cost of 0 into the home Storage (what
  does not fit waits on the party and the job ends), experience through `public.level_grant` at most once in `land_book_minutes`,
  and a company bonus as a chance of a second unit a turn rolled by `voyage.rng` (party, turn, Send).
- **Doors**: `cmd.land_send(action)`, `cmd.land_collect()`, `cmd.land_home()`, `cmd.land_tool(tool)` over one door shape
  (`public.land_door`) and four server-only cores that settle first. **Read**: `world.land(skill)`.
- **Knobs**: `land_enabled` **false**, `land_away_hours` 12, `land_passage_seconds_per_nm` 1.25, `land_yield_pct` 100,
  `land_level_speed_pct` 0.2, `land_pack_base` 6, `land_crowd_pct` 2, `land_crowd_max` 10, `land_book_minutes` 10.
- **Client**: the Land tab (`src/features/land/`: `LandScreen`, `PartyFace`, `SkillFace` with its Send tray, `landWords`), a
  `/land` route, a served-switch field on the tab table (`navTabs.ts`), the words in `src/lib/i18n/dict/land.ts` (EN and KO),
  `lvMining` / `lvAct_dig` / the gift-every line in `command.ts`, three refusal words in `codes.ts`, a `pick` icon in the kit, the
  five calls in the rpc catalogue, and the Levels tiles in two groups (Sea, Land) each with its own top and table while lit.

## Dark: what a player sees by this deploy

Nothing. `land_enabled` is false: every door refuses `E_NOT_IN_USE` before it writes, `world.land()` answers `{enabled: false}`,
`public.level_skills_card` serves no land skill, the bar draws the same six cells (the Land tab is `served: 'land_enabled'`), and
`world.snapshot().config` carries `land_enabled: false`. The only served differences are additive keys: `config.land_enabled` and,
on each sea skill of the Levels card, `side`, `top`, `gift_every` and `curve: null` (the client reads them; the tiles draw the same).
A typed `/land` URL prints "Not open yet."

**Rows a minute while dark: 0.** No job, no cron, no trigger; every door refuses before any write. **Lit:** 1 row a Send, 2 to 4 a
Collect (the party; a Storage row; `level_xp` and `level_book` at most once in 10 minutes), 2 a pick (party, ledger; a treasury row
while the purses are lit). The design's 7 holds: about 3.5 rows a minute at 100 companies, 35 at 1,000.

### What the design left "for the builder to measure" (5.4 point 5; 5.8 rule 2)

Read in the local world image, for a Lisbon company, each job at the level it asks, no tool, 6 packs, sold at Lisbon's bid
(`world.price`, which carries `port_goods.haul`):

- **Can the haul multiplier lift a far trip above digging at home?** Not above the rule's worth. Coin an hour at Lisbon's bid over the
  rule's worth at that level (4,800 to 15,000): far trips at most **0.91** (mean 0.21, 46 jobs), home jobs at most **0.90** (mean
  0.54, 11 jobs). No far trip pays more than the rule allows; the passage pace (1.25 s a mile) is left as it is.
- **One party-hour against a hundredth of the target stock** (rule 2) — **it does NOT hold for the cheap goods every region has**, at
  Lisbon: sand 133x, quartz 76x, stone 19x, feldspar 18x, lime 15x, gypsum 14.5x, clay 13.8x, salt 1.9x, cinnabar 1.3x, iron 1.1x the
  hundredth. Their Lisbon targets are tiny (sand 60, quartz 112) while the 40-s floor makes 80 units an hour. Every far job is under
  it. **Nothing is changed for this while dark** (a knob is the owner's): before lighting, either `land_yield_pct` goes down, the 40-s
  floor goes up for the cheapest goods, or the market's own brakes (the day's cap of 0.35 of the target a game-day, the glut, the
  floor of 0.35 of base) are accepted as the guard. Named here so it is decided before the switch, not after.

## Decided by default (the owner may reverse any)

1. **Mules are not in this slice** (cut by feature): packs are `land_pack_base` 6. Mules, `cmd.land_pack` and `land_packs` are the next
   land slice. Everything else of L1 shipped.
2. **The Repeat toggle is not built**: a party always repeats until the cap (the design's default). Rare finds (`land_drops`) are not
   in L1 (they were not in L1's tables).
3. **A tool is the party's, not a Storage item.** The design put the pick in `item_kinds` + `player_items` through a new
   `item_store`. In this repo `item_kinds` is the ships' fitting catalogue (slot, volume, workstation tier), `world-guard.mjs` holds it
   equal to `data/gear.generated.json`, and the Workshop and Codex list it: a pick there would show in live play while dark. So the
   tools are `public.land_tools` (catalogue) and `land_parties.tools` (what the party owns). `cmd.do_make` is not cut.
4. **Dark refuses with the repo's own code, `E_NOT_IN_USE`** ("Not in use."), as `cmd.buy_room` and the other dark doors do, not a
   new `E_LAND_DARK`. New codes, each with its EN and KO word: `E_LAND_OUT` (the party is out), `E_LAND_HAVE` (a tool it has),
   `E_NO_SUCH_JOB`. A level short is the repo's `E_NEEDS_SKILL` with have / need figures; no land in reach is `E_OUT_OF_REACH`; a
   haul waiting for room is `E_SHED_FULL`.
5. **No `-- news:` row**: the file says `-- news: none` (the land is dark; the News is told when the owner lights it).
6. **A site's name is a word of the client's dictionary** (`land.site_IBE_mines`, `land.site_BRI_mines`), not a column: the two
   written names and "Region · Mines" for the other 23. Region names are served by `world.land` (`regions`), as the client had no
   English region names.
7. **Level speed counts from level 1**: a job is `land_level_speed_pct` faster for each level above 1 (19.8 % at 100, not 20 %), so
   level 1 is 6.3's table exactly (Iron 45 s, a 5:00 trip).
8. **The ladder follows the catalogue's own regions** where 6.3's table differs (Rubies only in Eastern India; Quicksilver and
   Cinnabar are separate jobs with their own times by the rule: 6.3 averaged them).
9. **No foreign keys to goods, ports or regions** (static tables a deploy should not hold); asserted instead. The party's key to
   players is added last under the 5-s lock timeout, as 0411 did.
10. **The company bonus counts parties working that land at Send** (on passage or digging), and is frozen in the terms (design 5.4,
    point 4).
11. **The hunk to `client_rpc_entry_points` is anchored mid-list** (after `stats_now`), so another open PR appending at the tail does
    not move it.
12. **Storage full ends the job**: the party comes home with what does not fit and is not sent again until it is booked (6.2's table).
13. **The Land cell is between Map and Office** on the bar (design 6.7). Lit, the phone's bar is seven cells.

## Rollback

The knob: `land_enabled` false (owner only). Nothing to undo: a party out keeps its row and is booked at the first Collect after the
land is lit again. No forward file is needed; if the tables must ever go, a later migration drops them (they hold nothing while dark).
