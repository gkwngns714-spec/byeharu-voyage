## What it does, lit

- **A mark.** A battle whose country came too late or not at all, or whose attacker won, marks her: one row of `public.wanted`, 48 hours, a History line. The hall shows **Wanted 47 h** in that country's cities while it stands.
- **Frozen at departure.** A human voyage leaving with a Hostile regard or a mark writes `public.voyage_hostile`: the chance, one real ship, her sheet. That ship's waters are marked on the voyage's frozen segments. Nothing else is read later.
- **The day.** On a marked leg, one extra stream against the frozen chance, twice as likely when WANTED, once a voyage. The three old streams are drawn as ever, so no other day moves.
- **The meeting.** `public.fight_sides`, her fleet against the frozen sheet, no sizing up.

| Outcome | Goods | Hands | Fine |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boarded | 25-50 %, to the capital's stock | -10 % | 5 %, capped |
| Paid off | None | None | 5 %, capped |
| Thrown back, slipped | None | None | None |

Hull by the fight, never below 25 % of her size. Insurance pays what it covers, from the insuring city. The fine is `FINE`, moved, her purse to the capital. The navy's real ship takes her hurt; sinking her costs `regard_sink`.

**The Pacific answer, as built:** she beats Spain's ship off Callao and sails on. Spain marks her. For 48 hours any voyage she starts through Spain's waters may meet that navy. If her route keeps clear, nothing is written and nothing happens.

## The same day settled twice

Every figure in a NAVY day's payload is a function of the voyage, the day and the frozen row. Her hull and hands are her own, as on a PIRATES day. Proof 41 settles one voyage three ways, through the real fight: at once, day by day, and three days late after her ship was wrecked, unmanned and laid up, the regard forgiven and the knob at 0. The days and her ship read byte for byte alike. So does a mutation that re-reads the sheet go red.

**What is not in the payload:** what her purse really paid. The day says the fine levied. A purse that cannot cover it pays what it holds, no debt, and `FINE_PAID` and the ledger say what moved. So a late settle with a different purse moves different gold, under the same words.

## Decided by default (the owner may reverse)

From the brief and the doc: 48 hours; frozen at departure; fine 5 %, cap 100,000; the PLUNDERED table; goods to the capital's stock.

Decided here:
- **`navy_hunt_per_day` ships at 0, and it is the only switch.** The doc says 0 stops the hunt and names no lit value. No extra flag.
- **The mark is not a second rule of who is hunted.** A mark alone puts a voyage on the list, as the doc's "a WANTED row or a Hostile regard" says; it doubles the chance.
- **A dark regard hunts nobody,** a mark still does.
- **One ship a country, a voyage:** the station covering the most of her course, lowest ord on a tie. A leg belongs to one country, a WANTED country first.
- **Waters are sailed miles:** the seas of harbours within `navy_reach_nm` by `sea_reaches`, so an isthmus does not make Callao near Cartagena.
- **Her sheet is frozen with the row.** The doc says one real ship; the bar says settled twice is identical. She fights as she stood at departure, and her real hull takes the hurt.
- **Fine only on Boarded and Paid off.** The doc's table is PLUNDERED; Paid off had no rule, and a ship that pays with nothing fined made no sense.
- **Thrown back costs no hands.** The doc names only the plundered -10 %.
- **Cargo value is `goods.base_value`,** the one price that does not move, so the day stays pure. Not the market, not her cost.
- **A fine at sea buys back bad regard** at the fine's own price, capped at what is owed, as at the capital. It ends the mark when nothing is left owed. A part of one does not.
- **A NAVY day takes the place of that day's hazard.** One day, one kind.
- **`p_hazard` stays the sea's,** so the premium learns nothing of the hunt.
- **A divert is a new departure,** as it always was: the onward water is a new voyage, frozen as she turns.
- **The mark is written for any end with no ship in time,** a beaten attacker too: the doc says "too late or not at all".
- **`until` ends at the day's instant** for a fine at sea, at the moment of paying at a capital.
- `navy_hunt_wanted_mult` and `navy_crew_lost` are knobs the doc did not name.

## What the owner should see before lighting (not now)

1. **The lit value of `navy_hunt_per_day` is yours to set.** Arithmetic only: at 0.03 a day, ten days in a country's waters is about 26 %, about 46 % when WANTED.
2. **A country with no ship hunts nobody.** Only LIS, SVQ and SAK can pay for hulls today (W12's dev-log), so a Hostile company is safe from seven countries, and an attack on their cargo ships has no answer but regard.
3. **Attacking a navy ship is still not built.** `public.attack_for` refuses her, `regard_attack_navy` does not exist. W12 and W11 left it to W13 or W14; it is not in W14's row or this slice.
4. **A frozen ship can fight after she is gone.** A ship laid up or sunk after departure still meets the voyages frozen before it, for the length of those voyages. Her real hull stays at 0 until her next port patches her.
5. **`navy_hunt_per_day` 0 does not stop a voyage already frozen,** by the law of the slice. It stops new ones.
6. **100,000 d. at sea buys back up to 500 regard.** A fine can lift Hostile in one meeting. It is the doc's own price; a navy fine could instead buy nothing.
7. **Goods above a capital's `stock_target` fade at the next drift,** exactly as a sale above target does.
8. **A voyage report line is English only,** as every voyage line is. The Wanted row and the History line are EN and KO.
