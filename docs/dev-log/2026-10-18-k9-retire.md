## The aboard clock

0127: a voyage teaches only the captains aboard when it departed (`officer_xp.aboard_since`, else
`hired_at`). Since 0157 a captain is seated, moved or hired into a fleet only while it is docked
(D9: `seat_captain_for` checks both ships, `sign_officer` refuses a fleet at sea), and no function
moves a ship between fleets (`update public.ships … fleet_id` appears only as a filter in
`do_repair`, `fleet_load`, `settle`). So whoever sits in a fleet at sea sat there before she sailed,
and the clock added nothing. `captain_xp_credit` keeps "signed by then" (`hired_at <= p_since`);
`captain_seat_put` loses `p_came_aboard` (drop + create at three arguments, ACL asserted equal). The
migration asserts old filter = new for every captain seated in a sailing fleet.

## The first disposable-chain red, and its cause (run 39073875566)

(that runs after the comparison) and not the clock (`game_day` / `inn_shift` read `now()`, constant in the

are compared with that one array in a canonical order (every member and every field still equal), and NEW is
asserted to be in (job, name, code) order. The Goa move is now asserted as exactly its difference: every other
captain's odds at every harbour unchanged, both of theirs moved. The two Virji Voras are the owner's data (two
people, two roles) and are left as they are.

## The production push (as 0165) failed, and rolled back whole (prod stayed 0164)

`E_NOT_FOUND: there is no such merchant fleet` in the comparison block. Prod's plan (read with EXPLAIN,
read-only) pushed the card comparison — a qual on `fleets` alone — **below** the join to `players`, so it ran
for the 17 player fleets that `pl.is_npc` was meant to skip. The card refuses those. The CI world's plan happened
to evaluate it elsewhere. Fixed by comparing OUTCOMES over every fleet: `pg_temp.card_0166` returns the
card (officers in one order) or the refusal itself (`REFUSED <sqlstate>: <message>`), and OLD must equal NEW for
all of them — same card, or same refusal. Where the merchants are switched off (local and CI chains: every card
refused), the cards are compared again lit, inside a rolled-back block, so the answers are compared on every chain
(local: 850 refused alike; lit, 837 cards equal).

Run read-only on prod (a DO block under `transaction_read_only = on`, its result raised, so nothing commits): the
card over every fleet answers 837 and refuses 17 (all `P0001 E_NOT_FOUND`); every other reader the comparison calls
— world.inn at every inn, ship_seats for every ship, crew_terms for every fleet, promotion_next for every signing,
the seat picker for every seated signing, world.officers for nobody and for every signed-in company — answered all
3,045 calls with no refusal.
