## The three root causes

1. **The card lost its info.** #738 gave a tick battle's card a `lean` branch: only "A vs B", Watch and Join, and a press
   on the ring also opened the battle screen at once.
2. **Ships in a fight moved, on the client only.** The server holds every side (`public.battle_stop`: `voyage.hold` under
   sail, IN_BATTLE in port; table above). The map places the world's ships from `world.sea_roster`'s frozen facts, asked as
   a delta every 30 s, so the ship set upon sailed on for up to 30 s of a fight that lasts 1 to 73 s. Reproduced
   (tests/map.fightStill.spec.ts on the old build): the cargo ship moved 8.2 px in 6 s inside the ring. And the other way
   round: the roster serves a held fleet again while `busy_until` is set, and NOT when the battle closes and the hold is
   cut, so the map kept the 600 s hold: she lay still for ten minutes after a fight of one, then jumped.
3. **"No pirate."** A pirate strikes from where she is, within `attack_reach_nm` (240 nm), and every raider read was docked
   in her harbour. The circle is 30 nm round the ship set upon: the raider was never in it (old build: 1 red mark in the
   ring, the cargo ship; the pirate's mark at her harbour). Also: a battle stayed FIGHTING up to a minute past its last
   tick, its ring another 60 s after the close, and a navy fight's or her own raid's ring (settled in an instant) 5 minutes
   over water the ships had left; and at a wide zoom the merchant set upon could be one of the merchants the map thins out.

## What changed (client)

- **The card** (`src/live/FightSheet.tsx`): a tick battle's card is the card every fight has: Raid / Battle, "2 fleets · 3
  ships · Watching", **Watch (and Join) at the top**, then Attacker and Defender with each fleet's name, what she is and
  her ships; once it is over, the result. The ring opens the card and nothing else; the battle screen opens from Watch.
- **Every fleet of a fight stands at the fight** (`src/chart/fightPins.ts`): while a card is FIGHTING, each side that is
  there is drawn inside the circle and does not move: at her own served place when that is inside the circle, else beside
  the site (attackers east, defenders west). A fleet the map has no mark for is given one from the card. Never thinned.
- **The ring is a fight being fought** (`mapFightsOf`): it goes the moment the battle is over. A raid day and a navy fight
  settled in an instant draw no ring (decided by default; the read still serves them, and her raid is in her History).
- **A tick battle ends at its last tick** (`src/live/useFights.ts`): the map asks again at the card's `resolves_at`, and if
  the battle is still open it presses `cmd.battle_end` once (the design's door for any reader, tick-combat.md 6), so the
  ships are freed then and not up to a minute later. Load: no new rows; at most one call a battle for each open map
  (about 2 a minute at 116 raids an hour), doing what the minute job would have done.
- **She sails on** (`src/live/fightCuts.ts`): when a card is over, the kept roster row's hold is cut to the battle's own
  length and its eta moved back by the difference, the server's own arithmetic (`voyage.hold_cut`). A stopgap until 0444.
- **The stage is lower** (`src/live/battle/scene.ts`): the circle's middle is 62 % down the stage (was 54 %), no taller
  than the room under it. At 1280x800 the top ship's name is no longer under the sides' bars.

## Migration 0444 (branch `roster-after-fight`, NOT deployed)

`world.sea_roster`, one hunk: a delta also serves every fleet on a battle that closed since `p_since` less 3 minutes.
With it the server's own row (hold cut, eta right) reaches the map and `fightCuts.ts` does nothing. Proof 146 (fails
before at RS_CUT). Tested on the built image: applies, the proof passes after and fails before, a second apply refuses.
