## The fixes (0423)

| # | cause | knob | old | new |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | the rest stretched by the one-hour day | `route_rest_voyage_days` | 1.333333 (600 s) | **0.266667** (120 s at 192) |
| M2 | the planner never prices a loop past her 5 nearest | `npc_route_neighbours` | 5 | **8** |
| | | `npc_route_trade_candidates` | 6 | **10** |
| | | `npc_route_candidates` | 16 | **24** |
| M3 | a merchant refused 4 times waits 50 minutes while prices change every 15 | `npc_ask_wait_max_minutes` (new) | none (50) | **30** (0 = 0407's rule) |
| C1 | one cargo ship a capital a run | `convoy_loads_per_run` (new) | 1 | **10** |
| | | `convoy_load_max` (new) | no cap | **1,000,000** (0 = none) |
| N1 | a hunter lies six real hours | `navy_hunt_hours` | 6 | **0.0625** (half a voyage-day, 225 s) |
| N2 | her own sea the worst, she lies at her station | `navy_own_sea_patrol` (new) | (the lie) | **true**: she sails her station's patrol (false = the lie) |

Bodies cut (9 hunks, each once, each reversing): `public.npc_waiting_queue` (M3: the cap), `public.cargo_ships_load` (C1: the loop over
ships; a home gives its room ONCE A RUN however many ships load there, its room as the run first read it less each load, so no donor is
called past 0307's share a run: proof 32's donor scene holds unchanged), `public.navy_hunt_step` (N2). M1, M2 and N1 are knobs only. Each knob moves only from the value the file was written against.

**What stays (the guards):** a merchant sails with a BUY only when a lap of the loop nets above nothing after tax, wages, stores and every dock
fee (0186, 0406), each parcel no bigger than the sink's purse can pay (0410), and with nothing that pays she waits (0181). Proof 120's
AS_GUARD: the same scene with every purse at nothing, and she waits with no BUY. A broke house still waits the longest (50 minutes). A cargo
load is the old load (the same row lock, the same try of the purse, the same need less what is aboard) and money moves only through
`public.convoy_book` (CONVOY_LOAD, moved): AS_CARGO and the migration's probe check the realm's money before and after.

Decided by default (the owner may reverse any): 2 minutes of rest (the figure 0116 chose); 8 / 10 / 24 (half again as many loops; a laid-up
ask costs about half again as much, below); 30 minutes (two of 0420's reprices); 1,000,000 a ship (a realm short 4.6 M sends five ships, not
one: the funds reach the circuit from more homes and a raid takes less); a lie of half a voyage-day (225 s: judged against 0422, where a navy ship sails 4.5 times as fast, so a patrol leg takes under a third of what it did; a longer lie would be most of her time). NOT done: a laid-up house moving her home (a
ballast passage at a loss); a cargo ship sailing empty; Mongolia's 11 surplus ships (its number follows its purse, 0326); `port_prep_seconds`.
