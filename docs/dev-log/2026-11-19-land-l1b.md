# 2026-11-19 · Land L1b: the land keeps the market guard (migration 0457, proof 160) — DARK

Design `docs/design/land-2026-11.md` 5.8 rule 2: one party-hour of a land job is no more than **one hundredth of the good's target
stock at a middling harbour of its region**. L1 (0455) timed each job by price alone and measured that the guard did not hold
(`docs/dev-log/2026-11-18-land-l1.md`: at Lisbon sand 133x, quartz 76x, stone 19x, clay 14x the hundredth). This slice fixes the rule
in the generator and re-emits the 57 job rows. **Nothing is lit:** `land_enabled` stays false, `land_yield_pct` stays 100.

## The rule: what it was, what it is

- **Was (0455):** seconds = the time in which one unit is worth a party-hour's worth (4,800 coin at level 1 rising to 15,000 at
  level 100, at base value), to 5 s, never under 40 s. The market's size did not enter.
- **Is (0457), one rule in `scripts/build-land-migration.mjs`:** the most of (a) that worth's time, (b) 40 s, and (c) **the guard**:
  at the **fastest pace the game allows** (Mining 100 = 19.8 % faster, the best tool of `land_tools` = 3 %, `land_crowd_max` = a 10 %
  chance of a second unit a turn, `land_yield_pct` 100), one party-hour is at most a hundredth of the **least middling-harbour target
  over every region the good comes from**. Seconds rise by 5 s steps until that holds, with the server's own rounding of a turn
  (`public.land_terms`). Experience stays seconds x the pay a minute of the level: an hour of Mining pays the experience it paid.
- **Which target.** Every harbour has a market row for every good, and the brakes are fractions of that row's `stock_target`, so
  every harbour of the region counts. A region's figure is its middling harbour (the lower median, a real harbour's figure). The
  target taken is **the least the market's own depth rule can hold a row at**: its base (`base_target`, or `stock_target` where no
  multiple was ever applied) x `depth_m0` (0.5). Why: on production the depth is lit (0252): the first prod read of this slice found
  clay at Oceania's middling harbour at 33.13, not the chain's 60 (Fremantle, Darwin, Port Chalmers, Midway and Honolulu at 30 =
  60 x 0.5). Guarded against the day's `stock_target`, 0457 would have failed its self-assert on production, and a day of
  disinvestment could break the guard again. Base x 0.5 is the same figure in the chain's world and on production, and no day can
  go under it.
- **The input** is `data/land-guard.generated.json`, written by `scripts/db/measure-land-guard.mjs` from the chain's world up to 0454
  (no land file touches a market). **The proof** is three-sided: the generator raises seconds until the guard holds; 0457's
  self-assert checks every job in every region through `public.land_terms` on the live rows of the world it is applied to; proof 160
  checks the same and that the check can fail.
- A march, a passage or fewer packs only slow a party, so the turn is the bound: **mules cannot break the guard** (proof 160,
  GUARD_PACKS: iron with 30 packs is 7.16 an hour, under the turn's 7.17).

## What it does to a new company's first ten minutes (design 6.1)

Read through `public.land_terms` for a fresh company of Lisbon at Mining 1, no tool (proof 160, GUARD_FIRST).

| | 0455 (the design's 6.1) | 0457 |
|---|---|---|
| Iron, a trip of 6 at home | 5:00 (45 s a turn) | **1:05:00** (645 s a turn) |
| First haul home (pressed at 0:10) | 5:10, Mining 3 (24 experience) | **1:05:10**, Mining 16 (324 experience) |
| By minute 10 | two iron hauls, Mining 5, Stone open | **nothing home**; the tray says "6 a trip, 1:05:00" |
| Sea Coal by Plymouth, a trip of 6 | 36:10 | **1:01:40** (passage 15:50 each way, 295 s a turn) |
| Clay / Stone / Sand, a trip of 6 | 4:30 | **28 h 17 min** (over the 12-hour cap: the one trip comes home whole) |
| Experience an hour, iron, level 1 | 288 | 299 (the same rule; marches are a smaller share) |
| Coin an hour, iron at base value 60 | about 4,320 | about **332** |

- **London** (iron and coal at home): coal 30:00 a trip, iron 1:05:00; the first Iron Bars after about 1 h 35 (6.1 said "by minute ten").
- **Karachi** and the other capitals whose first day was "Clay and Sand, which lie everywhere": a trip of either is now 28 h 17 min.
- So in the first ten minutes **the land gives a new player one press and a wait**, no haul and no level. The level pace an hour is
  as designed (experience scales with the seconds); the goods and coin an hour are what the guard allows. Clay, Stone, Sand, Lime,
  Gypsum, Feldspar and Quartz become jobs nobody will choose: their quays hold 30 to 42 at the depth floor, so a hundredth is a
  third of a unit an hour.

**The readings the owner can choose between** (decided by default: the first, the strict one; each is one constant in the generator):

| Reading of rule 2 | Iron, Lisbon, a trip of 6 | Clay |
|---|---|---|
| Fastest pace, base x depth floor (**shipped**) | 1:05:00 | 28 h 17 min |
| Fastest pace, today's chain `stock_target` (fails on PROD today: 1.81x) | 33:00 | 14 h 9 min |
| The level asked, no tool, no crowd, base x depth floor | 46:30 | 20 h |

Or keep the strict guard and let the cheap goods leave the first minutes (Iron and Coal lead; Clay's job is the far trip of last
resort), or raise the cheap goods' quay targets (a market change, not a land one). None of these is done: **the owner's**.

## Decided by default (the owner may reverse any)

1. **The guard holds at the fastest pace, not at the level asked.** A guard that a level-up, a tool or a crowd can break is not a
   guard. The fastest pace is a constant in the generator (`FASTEST`), and 0457's self-assert reads the live knobs and tools: a
   faster tool (L2's iron to wootz picks, 6 to 30 %) or a knob raised over these figures needs the rows re-emitted.
2. **The target is base x `depth_m0`**, the least the market can be held at, not the day's figure (above). If the owner lowers
   `depth_m0`, the guard needs re-emitting too.
3. **Every harbour of a region counts** (all 254 x 629 rows are markets with the same brakes), not only those whose roster offers it.
4. **Proof 158 pins Iron 45 s / 4 and Sea Coal 40 s / 3** inside its own rolled-back transaction: it proves the sum's mechanics on the
   design's hand-worked figures; proof 160 proves the rows.
5. **The scratch `scripts/db/proofs/999_measure_guard.sql` is deleted**; its measurement lives on as `scripts/db/measure-land-guard.mjs`.

## Not in this slice (not started)
