## Deploy notes (for the coordinator)

- Deploy with the clock unwound (`unwind_the_clock()` → `db push` → `wind_the_clock()`).
  - The migration writes `players`, `port_influence` and `port_mayor`.
  - It also takes ACCESS EXCLUSIVE on `players` for the foreign-key swap.
- Push the DB first, then the client. A client ahead of the DB would read `nations[].great` as
  missing, and its country picker would be empty.
- After deploy, read three things:
  - the migration's notices: the companies per power, the cities held and the re-key;
  - the first 00:47 UTC receipt of `tick_influence` (`claimed`, `changed`);
  - W.
