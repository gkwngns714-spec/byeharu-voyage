## What lands (everything behind `public.pirates_on()`)

- **A pirate merchant** is a merchant house (`npc_houses`) of nation PIR, founded through `public.npc_found` like the ten crowns' 821, with ONE difference in ONE place: her starting money comes from the hoard through THE one payer `public.pirate_pay` (a hunk in `public.founding_credit`, the one place a company's starting money is paid), inside THE one cap `public.pirate_spend_room`, as the new moved kind `PIRATE_FOUND`. A founding the hoard cannot pay is refused WHOLE (`E_HOARD_SHORT`): nothing of her stays written. The hunk runs before the open-economy branch: a dark hoard pays nothing, so no pirate merchant is ever minted, whatever the economy's state.
- **The job** `public.tick_pirate_merchants` (pg_cron `byeharu-voyage:pirate-merchants`, every 10 minutes, never in the beat): dark, one knob read. Lit, at most ONE founding a run (`public.pirate_found`), each gate said in its answer: `full` (`pirate_merchants_max` 30), `paced` (the newest founded inside `pirate_found_hours` 2), `no_berth` (`npc_fleets` at `npc_fleet_max`: she would lie paused 'dark', 0105), `no_leg` (no home with a known leg), `room` (`pirate_spend_room` under her capital PLUS what the ships are owed for the rest of the UTC day, `pirate_size x navy_upkeep_day` pro rata, so a founding never starves W17's hourly pay), `refused` (the payer, a name, a route: rolled back whole and said).
- **Her spec** (`public.pirate_merchant_spec(slot)`, a read): slot = the count of pirate merchants + 1; one of thirty names of the era's pirate coasts (the Caribbean, the Barbary coast, the wokou of Kyushu, the lanun of the Strait); her home the capital for slot 1 then the homes by code, wrapping, the first in the ring with a known leg out; `pirate_merchant_class` (`carlat`, the lateen caravel of the Barbary corsairs); `pirate_merchant_capital` 250,000 d.; Haggling 1 (tuition 2,500 d., paid to her home's academy harbour: moved); no officer; patience 0.30; wares none (the planner's "every good"); ink `pir`; a two-stop first loop out and back to the nearest known harbour of her home. From there she is a merchant like any: tended, re-planned, trading, paying wages and dock dues, refitted from her home's purse (0322).
- **Investing**: through the one pass (`public.npc_invest`, nothing changed): her stake and gift land in `port_influence` under PIR; `tick_influence` ranks PIR with the ten as any nation. Dark she invests and gives nothing (a hunk in `npc_invest_roster`). She is never gifted by the capital's pass (a hunk in `capital_gift_roster`).
- **Nobody insures her** (a hunk in `insure_voyage`): `{insured: false, why: 'pirate'}`, no premium to any city, no payout from any.
- **The limits of 7.6**, lit WITH the pirates: (a) the planner skips a PIRATE HOME as a destination unless it is the merchant's own home (three hunks in `npc_loop_candidates` through one reader `public.npc_legs_open`); (b) `pirate_port_day_cap` 0.05 of a good's most stock (twice the target: W17's stow ceiling) a day a company at a pirate home (one hunk in `world.daily_cap_remaining`, the one reader of the cap, so Buy, Sell, the fulfil order, the good's board and the merchant leg planner all read it).
- Two readers: `public.pirate_home(port)` (a pirate home: allegiance home under the pirate power; `port_is_free` is this AND the lever) and `public.pirate_merchant(player)` (an `npc_houses` row whose company sails for the pirate power; a pirate ship's house is not one). The ink `pir` joins the eight (the check on `npc_houses.ink`). Five knobs, one word, one cron job. Nothing is minted: W is unchanged by every move (proved).

## Decided by default (the owner may reverse any)

- **The hull is a caravel, not a xebec.** `npc_found` lays only a hull a merchant loadout is authored for (`npc_loadouts`: barca, carlat, nau); a xebec refuses `E_NO_LOADOUT`. The lateen caravel is the Barbary corsairs' own hull, so it is the default; the knob names any of the three (or a line hull of them).
- **Her first loop is out and back to the nearest known harbour of her home**, from the courses the world holds (`npc_known_legs`): Port Royal to New York (1,452 nm), Djerba to Tripoli, Goto to Nagasaki, Lingga to Malacca. The design's "among PIR ports and free cities" cannot be sailed as written: no course joins two pirate homes, and the free cities near a home are what the courses reach. The planner re-plans her at her first lap like every merchant, from her home, over every harbour that is no pirate home (hers kept as her way back), and she trades anywhere she docks. Her business is fencing the home's plunder (W17's stow) abroad.
- **"Unless she is PIR" is read as "except her own home."** The only pirate home a pirate merchant's loop can hold is her own, so the two rules are one, and the cheaper one (no read of the merchant, one read of the homes a call) is kept. A crown merchant today sailing a loop through Port Royal (12 do) keeps it until a candidate pays more: the planner stops OFFERING the home; it does not tear up a loop.
- **A pirate merchant is never gifted** by the capital's pass: the hoard pays her founding and nothing after. Routing the gift through `pirate_pay` (within the cap) is one more hunk if the owner wants a broke pirate merchant refilled.
- **Dark after lit**: a pirate merchant already founded goes on trading when the switch is turned off (she is a merchant; laying her up is a decision the owner can ask for), but the pirates invest and give nothing while dark.
- **The ships come first in the day's room**: a founding needs the room to hold her capital AND the ships' pay for the rest of the UTC day, and never more than one in two hours. With the hoard at Port Royal's 993 M d. the room is about 4.97 M a day, the ships (14 at that hoard) need about 0.84 M, so about 12 foundings a day fit and 2 hours spaces them: the roster's 24 berths fill in two days.
- **Only a pirate HOME is skipped and capped**, not a city the pirates win: plunder lands in a home's stock (W17's stow), and that is where the arbitrage is.
- **The ink is `pir`**, the pirates' nation code lower-cased as the eight families are (`prt`, `esp`, ...). It names her kind on the chart (`shipKindOf`); the server marks no kind on her row, because she IS a merchant (`fleet_kind` MERCHANT, every engine read of a merchant unchanged).
- **Her look is one colour day and night** (`--color-pirate-hull` #ff9999, a pale red) with a near-black X, not the design's "red by night, near-black by day": the kind table (W0a) has one fill token a kind, the same by day and by night (the owner's "unify the color"), and the pale red is bright enough to stand on the day's deep water and the dusk's mixed sea where the dark keyline cannot carry her. Checked by `tests/map.kinds.spec.ts` (3 : 1 on every ground, every twilight step, the mark on her fill, a hue apart from cargo teal and navy violet).
- **The card says "Pirate merchant" before her name**, from her served ink, as a cargo or navy ship's card says its kind. No Codex card: `world.stats_now` serves no count of pirate merchants, and a count is a read for another slice.
- **The job is its own** (`byeharu-voyage:pirate-merchants`, every 10 minutes), not a hunk in W17's `tick_pirates`: W17's body was being written the same day by another hand, a founding needs no minute cadence, and a job of its own is the pattern (cargo ships, navy, battles, pirates each have one). Dark it costs one knob read every 10 minutes.
- **Not changed, named**: the anonymous pirate hazard of a voyage and W17's named raider apply to a pirate merchant's voyage as to any merchant's (the engine reads `is_npc`, not the flag). A pirate ship raiding a pirate merchant is a question for W17/W19 (one line in `voyage.freeze_raiders` or `pirate_ship_in_sea` if the owner wants the Brethren to spare their own). Regard is unaffected by anything here.
