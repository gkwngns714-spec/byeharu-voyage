## 2. The fix (0448)

The definitions are in `docs/STATS_ONE_COUNT.md` (decided by default): five exclusive states in one order (laid up, wrecked, in battle, at
sea, in port); "In battle" shown as its own state; retired cargo ships hidden everywhere; the five count fleets, the faces say "Fleets",
"Ships" and "By fleet"; every state is the count's, written by one statement.

- New, server-only: `public.fleet_state_of` (the rule), `public.fleet_census` (the facts of every fleet), `public.fleet_state` (one fleet),
  `public.fleet_tally` (the count).
- 14 hunks over 7 bodies: `stats_fleets_count`, `stats_fleet_list_rows`, `tick_stats` (one statement), `stats_merchant_fleets`,
  `world.stats_fleets`, `world.stats_merchants`, `world.stats_fleet`. No body of the Stats holds a rule of its own any more.
- The newest count is re-stated once at the deploy, so the faces are whole at once and not at the next count.
- Client: `fleetStates.ts` (the five words, once) and `FleetStatesBar.tsx` (the one drawing), used by the Ships, Merchants, Navy, Pirates
  and Cargo faces; "In battle / 전투 중", "By fleet / 선단별", "Wrecks / 난파 횟수"; the Merchants face says "Counted".
