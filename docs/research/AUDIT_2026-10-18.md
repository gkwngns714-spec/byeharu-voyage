# Research audit — 2026-10-18

**Why.** The coordinator told the owner from memory that Uncharted Waters Origin "has about 7 nations". It has
eight. The owner: *"if you are wrong i am 의심 on everything you've made research."* So every factual claim in
`docs/research/` was re-opened against the URL it cites.

**Scope.** `UWO_TRADE_PRICES.md` (193 lines), `UWO_SKILLS_AND_CAPTAINS.md` (826 lines), `EVE_SKILLS.md` (684 lines),
plus the nation claim in `docs/DESIGN_RESEARCH.md:29`. Line numbers below are those of commit `440dfb8`, i.e.
**before** the inline `[audit 2026-10-18]` notes this PR adds.

**Method.** Each cited URL was fetched again (WebFetch; the two rate-limited Steam guides by direct download) and
asked, neutrally, what it says about the claim. Korean pages were read in Korean. Two Fable helpers did the long
tails (EVE; UWO §1–3.3, §3.5–3.13, §4.1–4.3, §4.6–4.9, §5–6); their WRONG verdicts were spot-checked before being
written here. Every verdict in the table is from a page read this session; nothing is carried over from the
original research.

**Statuses.** CONFIRMED — the source says it. WRONG — the source contradicts it, or the quotation / citation is not
on the page (the correct fact is in the last column). UNSUPPORTED — the cited page does not say it and no other
source was found. DISPUTED — sources disagree or the claim is a derivation the source does not make. UNVERIFIED —
no source could be reached this session.
