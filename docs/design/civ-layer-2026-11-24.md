## What "Civilization, online" means here
You do not rule a nation. You grow ONE place (your HQ in your home city), decide what is built and who does what, adopt policies, and
your country's players build together. Always-on real time instead of turns.

**Unchanged laws:** no PvP, no conquest, no world reset, closed economy, HQ is where you act. The stance table stays data.

## Slices (after 0501; each dark, its own after-check)
1. **K1 `next-up`** (client, inside H1's page, after H1 and H2). A strip of the three goals nearest done, from served figures only: next Rank, next
   crew slot, next skill level, next estate. Research: staggered goals are what makes "one more turn".
2. **K2 `hq-build-queue`**: part of 0499 estates, not a second system. Estates are buildings in a queue: one builds at a time, up to
   three wait, the price taken when its build starts. HQ draws the buildings you own, growing by tier.
3. **K3 `charter`** (0502, `charter_enabled`): **policies paid for with honour.** The owner, 2026-11-24: *"it will not be a turned base,
   but policies sounds good, with honourthe reputation ? honor thing"*. Read by default as: **honour IS Reputation**, the balance that
   exists (`reputation_credit`, 0352). No second honour currency.
   - Slots on Rank: 1 at Rank 2, then 2 / 3 / 4 at Rank 4 / 7 / 10 (rows of `level_perks`).
   - Adopting a policy SPENDS Reputation, once, through `reputation_credit`; dropping one refunds nothing. This replaces the free
     change on a Rank rise; a change is still at most once a real day a slot.
   - A policy is a trade-off (say, upkeep -10 % and XP -5 %) read through the existing cost and perk readers; the 50 % floor holds.
   - Stronger policies cost more Reputation. They compete with captain skills and crew slots for it: that is the choice. Prices are set
     against the measured Reputation income (`rep_day_cap` 600 a real day) in the 0502 slice, before it is lit.
   - Open for the owner: whether honour should also FALL for bad conduct (attacking ships, failed contracts). Not built by default;
     a country's regard (0319) already falls for attacks.
4. **K4 `country-works`** (0503, `country_works_enabled`). One shared work a country at a time, funded by citizens with coin (to the
   city purse) and goods (from their sheds). Finished, it gives every citizen a small lasting benefit and names its builders. Target
   is scaled to citizens active in the last 7 days, so one or two players can finish one.
5. **K5 `catch-up`**: crew slot 4 and above cost less for companies below the median slot count of active companies (one factor, floor
   75 %), folded into 0497 and its switch. From Politics and War's indexed city price.

no closure cause was verified); expelling idle players; attack windows; nations that need a crowd (19 companies, few active).
