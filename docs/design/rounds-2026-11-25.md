# Rounds: one shape for sea combat, sea expedition and land expedition (2026-11-25)

**DESIGN ONLY. Nothing is built, no production read was made.** Work order item 6; the owner's red comments R3, R5, R6 and section 2's rows
`rounds`, `ammo`, `wrecks-yield`, `artifacts` (`owner-spec-red-2026-11-25.md`). Every figure is a row of data and **decided by default**; the
owner may reverse any. Code was read on origin/main (0497): 0455 (the land party), 0435 (`tick_fight`), 0327/0374 (`loot_due`), 0339 (the
pirates' hoard, `pirate_pay`, `pirate_stow`), 0234 (`capital_pays`), 0357 (`battle_rounds`). Names marked NEW do not exist.

## 1. What the owner asked

- **R3** An expedition is a repeating activity with a log. Each round spends food, water and crew; at a high enough level it also gives
  provisions. It stops at MIN crew or the number set, then the nearest city with the goods. Gold, RP, artifacts (uncommon to very rare).
- **R5** Combat: send a fleet to a sea, destroy the pirates, that is one round. Show ammo, provisions and crew a round, **not odds**. At the
  provisions minimum she goes to the nearest city, restocks, then repeats or goes on, by the setting. Gold, RP and WRECKS, no honor badge.
- **R6** RUN is for trading and expedition fleets that are attacked. A fleet sent to fight has none.

## 2. The one shape

A **round** = `{cost, yield, log line}`, in one table of data and one state row. No round is an event, an order or a job step.

| Data row, `round_works` (NEW) | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `code`, `kind` (`combat`, `sea`, `land`), `sea` or `place`, `skill`, `level` | what it is, where, and the level it asks |
| `seconds` | a round's real time |
| `food`, `water` (days of stores), `ammo` (units a gun) , `crew_pct` | the cost; crew loss falls with the skill's level |
| `gold`, `rp`, `salvage` (goods and weights), `artifact` (tier and weight), `provisions` from a level | the yield |

| State, `round_runs` (NEW): one row a worker that is out | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `worker` (fleet or party), `work`, `sent_at`, `booked` (rounds booked) | as `land_parties` keeps `started_at` and `booked` |
| `terms` | frozen at Send: the cost and yield a round, speed of rounds, the stop rule, the sea's level |
| `stop` | `{crew, food_days, rounds, then: repeat or home}`, from the settings the player pressed |
| `carry` | gold due, RP due, salvage, artifacts: in hand until she makes a city |
| `log` | the last 20 lines, in the row (section 5) |

**Retention:** bounded by construction, one row a worker (6 fleets and a few parties a company), deleted when she is home and booked.

## 3. One settle, from time passed

`public.round_settle(worker, at)` (NEW) is 0455's `land_settle` widened, not a copy: the land party's settle becomes its caller for `land`
works, so there is one arithmetic of "how many rounds have passed, where is she, what is booked".

1. `n = least(floor((at - start) / seconds), what the stop rule allows, the 12-hour away cap) - booked`.
2. The stop rule is **arithmetic on the terms**: the first round after which crew, food, water or ammo would be under its minimum. So a late
   settle and a prompt one give the same answer.
3. Book `n` rounds in one write: take the costs from the hull's own stores and hold, add the yield to `carry`, keep the last lines.
4. Dice are `voyage.rng(world secret, worker, round, 'round:' || sent)`, as the land's, so the same press gives the same result.
5. Past the stop: she sails to the **nearest city** by `fleet_job_legs` (0494: harbour by harbour, the sea gate kept), sells nothing, books
   `carry` there, buys stores and ammo, hires to the set crew and repairs (0516's settings lines), then `repeat` or stays.

Called by her own reads and presses, and by `tick_fleet_jobs` when a run's stop time passes. **No new cron job and no beat step.** While she
is out she holds no voyage: `fleets.status` is the state `fleet_state_of` already calls at sea, with her sea as position.

**Offline is never worse than watching:** the settle is the only writer, so closing the page costs nothing.

## 4. The three kinds

### 4.1 Sea combat (R5, R6): `tick_fight` fights it, and nothing else does

- At Send the server plays `public.tick_fight(seed, her hulls, the pirates of that sea, opts)` on **16 seeds once** and freezes the 16 results
  in `terms` (hull lost, hands lost, shots fired, won or beaten off). A round picks one by its dice. So 12 hours of rounds cost 16 fights
  (about 16 x one call, to be measured), not one a round.
- The 16 are played again when her worst hull has crossed a 10 % step, or her ammo or crew has fallen a quarter: weak fleets fight worse.
- **The screen shows the mean cost of a round** (ammo, food, water, crew, hull) and what it brings. **No chance of winning is served**: the 16
  outcomes stay on the server. **No RUN**: the opening stance is FIGHT (`a_flee` off); a round that turns against her ends BEATEN OFF, the run
  stops and she goes to the nearest city with what she has.
- RUN and Evasion stay where they are: a trading or expedition fleet that is raided (0432, 0450, 0453), untouched.
- It is **NOT a second combat system**: the same hulls, abilities, armour, crits and raid results; no new damage figure anywhere.
- Never against a company, a merchant or a navy; the target is the sea's pirates alone.

### 4.2 Sea expedition (R3)

Each round spends food, water and, by the level, some crew. A level of Exploration 8 or more: one round in four brings provisions. Gold, RP,
artifacts. If she is attacked the raid is today's raid, RUN allowed, and the run stops until it is over.

### 4.3 Land expedition

The same shape with the party (0455) as worker: the captain, the crew cap by Combat level and the badges are `land-expedition`, `captain-works`
and `honor-badge`, which come after and only add columns to `terms`. Today's mining and smithing keep their exact terms.

## 5. The log, inside the database budget

- A company out 12 hours with 10-minute rounds is 72 rounds. A row each for 60 workers would be about 4,300 rows a day, for nothing.
- So the log is `round_runs.log`: a jsonb list of **at most 20 lines**, about 2 KB, one line a round, the older ones folded into totals
  (`rounds`, `crew lost`, `gold`, `salvage`). At the return ONE event row (`ROUNDS_DONE`, the totals) is written, as a voyage's report is.
- **Expected:** one update of one row a press or a stop; at most 1 event and 1 ledger row a run home. 0 rows a minute while nobody presses.
  Fillfactor is the default; the row is under 4 KB and bounded, so autovacuum's scale factor is untouched.
- Budget line for the migration: "no cron, about 6 row updates a worker a day, 1 to 3 events".

## 6. Ammo

- **Ammo is the goods Gunpowder and Shot and Ball**, in the hold, taking hold space like any good (catalogue ids `gunpowder`, `shot-and-ball`).
- A round spends `ammo` units of each a gun that fired (`tick_fight`'s own shots), at the quarter unit a volley, so 100 guns over a long round
  spend about 20 of each. Fewer aboard: the guns fire in proportion and the round is worse. None: no round starts, the stop rule ends it.
- Bought and sold at the quay as today; a fleet restocks at the nearest city to the amount set (`ammo` setting, BUY lines, limits by 0516).
- **No raid or battle of today spends ammo.** Only rounds do. (Question 2.)

## 7. Salvage (screen word **Wrecks**)

- A won round yields parts of the beaten hull: planks, cordage, canvas, iron, and gear parts. **Moved, never minted**: `public.salvage_take` (NEW)
  takes the units out of the **pirate homes' own stock** (`port_goods` of the Brethren's cities, which 0339's plunder fills), under the same
  shape as `loot_due`: a share (`salvage_share`), a cap a ship, a cap a company a day, and never more than is there. An empty stock yields none.
- **Gold:** from the Brethren's hoard through `public.pirate_pay` (its own day's room), through 0327's caps and 0374's rule "what it cost you". The
  sum is: hoard down, her purse up, to the coin. A hoard with no room pays nothing that round, and the log says so.
- Salvage goes to her hold (space), then Storage at the city. **Uses:** the yard takes the planks, canvas and cordage as the goods they are;
  the Forge takes gear parts in its recipes. So the parts are ordinary goods or `items`; no new currency, no new Forge. Ready-made ships or gear
  from a wreck come later by the owner's word.
- The player's own WRECKED fleet (#868) is another thing: code says `salvage` everywhere, the screen says Wrecks.

## 8. Artifacts

Four tiers as data: **uncommon, rare, very rare** (and none, 94 % of rounds). Default weights a round: 4 %, 1.5 %, 0.5 %, doubled at level 15.
She carries them home in the hold (1 space). Reported at her country's capital: gold by tier (400, 2,500, 15,000) from the **capital's purse** by
`public.capital_pays` (its crown call if short; if the realm is short, RP is paid and the gold waits), and RP (15, 60, 300). Special gear and
ships: LATER (the owner's word). Each artifact is a row of the company's `player_items` (a plain item), so there is no new table.

## 11. What is measured before any light

1. `tick_fight` x 16 at Send: milliseconds on 100 real sheets (target under 400 ms, one call at a time, no job).
2. The settle: 200 workers out, each settled once: ms and rows (target 1 update each).
3. The equality of the land's settle before and after (every terms row, 10,000 instants).
4. **Gold an hour** of a combat fleet against a trading fleet of the same tier and level (target 60 to 120 % of the trade; loot risk 0374 as is).
5. The hoard's and the capitals' purses after three simulated days; the world's sum to the coin, lit and dark.
6. Crew loss a day against what a company can hire (target: a new company can run 4 hours without a bankrupt hire).
7. `round_runs` row size and the hot set; the gate read before and 30 minutes after each light.

## 12. Questions for the owner (at most three)

1. **May a fleet sent to combat keep fighting until it is BEATEN OFF, or should it leave when the hull is under a figure you set?** Default: it
   stops at 60 % of the hull, changeable on the sheet (as 0516's damage stop).
2. **Should today's raids and battles also spend Gunpowder and Shot and Ball?** Default: no, only the rounds, so nothing a company does today
   costs more.
3. **What should an artifact be worth at the top?** Default: 15,000 gold and 300 RP for a very rare one, about one in 200 rounds.
