# Roadmap, 2026-11-23: from a trading sheet to a whole game

The owner, 2026-11-23: *"This game is a mixture of EVE Online, Uncharted Waters, RuneScape 3, and Melvor Idle (Jagex). Add/change/remove
what seems necessary to make this game a whole lot better."*

This builds on `docs/design/content-plan-2026-11.md` (2026-11-16), `docs/design/land-2026-11.md`, `docs/LEVELS.md` and the three play-tests in
`docs/design/playable-2026-11-22.md` (sections 1, 5 and 6). Prod head at writing: **0473**. Every choice below is **decided by default**;
the owner may reverse any.

---

## 1. The short version

**The game has almost every system the four reference games have.** What it lacks is the thread that leads a newcomer from one to the
next, numbers she can trust, and a reason to come back tomorrow. So the first five slices are about the first hour, and they mostly
*join* systems that exist rather than add new ones. Removals come early, because a newcomer meets 33 buttons on the first phone screen.

**The first five, in order:**
1. One honest profit number (card = sheet = arrival card = purse).
2. The second ship is a real goal (no gift loophole, a working purse left, a coin bar to aim at).
3. A job is one press (the hold jobs on the Trade desk, and a press that plans the trip).
4. A real choice and a full hold (two or three near harbours, a second good to fill the hold).
5. The clear-out: one place for each thing (the removal list, section 4).

**No human has sailed since 2026-10-30.** So "better" means, first, *a new player stays past the first hour*. Mid-game depth (mastery,
weekly orders, industry, nations) comes after, and only on systems the first hour has already shown her.

## 3. The four reference games, against this game

| Reference | What makes it work | What this game has | What is missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EVE Online** | A player-driven economy; regional markets where information is the edge; industry chains; contracts; real loss and insurance; corporations; territory. | Regional prices that drift and react; 869 houses trading; insurance; the fight; city influence by nation. | You cannot trust the profit figure (three numbers for one trip); every card sells at one harbour (no arbitrage choice); no contracts between companies; loss never happens to a newcomer; industry exists but has no use. |
| **Uncharted Waters** | A nation and home port picked at start; guild jobs (deliver X to Y) that set the destination; port discovery; fleet growth; trade, adventure and battle fame. | Nation pick; capitals; hold jobs; Exploration level; the yard; fame-style titles. | Jobs are four taps deep and cannot be sailed to; a new port is just a line on the arrival card; the second ship is bought in a loophole. |
| **RuneScape 3** | Many skills with loud level-ups; quests with story; always a clear goal; daily/weekly challenges; a collection log. | 11 sea levels with unlocks, land skills to 100, the goal card, Reputation. | Level-ups are quiet; no page says "fastest way to the next level"; no weekly goals; no collection log; no story (owner row 311 holds quests). |
| **Melvor Idle** | Progress while away; mastery per item; clean skill loops; numbers that go up; automation unlocks. | While away card; standing routes; land party up to 12 h; level curve. | No mastery per good; the second ship does not automate anything (it joins the first fleet and sails empty); land and sea do not feed each other yet. |

**Four rules taken from them** (adding to the two-week-old four in playable 2):
1. **The number she sees is the number she gets** (EVE's markets are cold but honest).
2. **Every new thing unlocks the next** (Melvor, RS3): second ship → a route that runs by itself → a third ship.
3. **A job sets the destination** (Uncharted Waters).
4. **Something is always counting up toward a reward, also while away** (Melvor mastery, RS3 collection log).

## 5. The roadmap: eighteen slices

Server slices take the next migration number in order (0474 onward), each dark behind its own `world_config` switch, one at a time,
lit after its deploy and a 30-minute after-check (the owner's standing order). Client slices go in parallel. Sizes: S about a day,
M two or three, L a week or more.

**Database budget for all of it:** prod is the smallest size, about 740 MB of 1,500, beat about 150 ms. No slice here adds a job that runs
every minute over every ship. New tables are per company and small (rows listed with each). A slice that adds a cron line or a table
carries its `-- budget:` line.

### The first hour (slices 1-5)

**1. One honest profit number.** *Change.* EVE (fees shown up front).
- What: the card, the Buy & sell sheet, the arrival card and the purse show the same figure for a trip. The card takes off the dock fee,
  wages and the profit tax as the sheet does; the arrival card prints the trip's net (served on the sale event); Rank Week counts only
  trade and voyage rows, not gifts or hull orders.
- Server + client. Migration 0474, switch `honest_profit_enabled`. Size S.
- Player effect: she trusts the game's numbers from trip one (play-test 3 #1, #28).
- Load: none (same rows, one more figure on an existing event). Depends on: nothing.

**2. The second ship is a real goal.** *Change + remove the gift loophole.* Uncharted Waters (fleet growth), Melvor (a bar that fills).
- What: the gift pass counts what she has paid for hulls on order; the order takes a deposit (the fee now, the rest at launch) or refuses
  when it would leave her under 20,000, and says why. The goal card reads "Second ship · 46,112 / 52,000" with a bar and opens the yard on
  the Order; Order is the gold button while materials are missing; the button says "Short 5,888"; a confirm says what is left.
- Server + client. Migration 0475, switch `order_deposit_enabled`. Size S-M.
- Player effect: a clear 20-40 minute goal she earns, and she can still trade after ordering (play-test 3 #2, #3, #5, #6).
- Load: none. Depends on: 1 (so the bar counts real profit).

**3. A job is one press.** *Change.* Uncharted Waters (guild jobs set the destination).
- What: the nearest open hold job sits as one line on the Trade desk under the goal card. "Buy at Bristol" is a press that plans the trip
  the way Buy & sell does (sail there, buy, bring it back), using the route builder that exists. The job's pay shows on the arrival card.
- Client only (the source harbour is already served since 0473). Size M.
- Player effect: a second kind of trip in the first ten minutes, with a destination chosen for a reason (play-test 3 #4).
- Depends on: nothing.

**4. A real choice and a full hold.** *Change.* EVE (regional arbitrage), Patrician (fill the hold with several goods).
- What: among near sales of similar profit an hour, the cards spread over two or three harbours; a desk line "From here: Setubal +2,982 ·
  Porto +3,9xx · Seville +4,7xx"; the Buy & sell sheet offers a second good for the same harbour with the room left; the near rule ends
  after two hours played or three ships, not at Trading 5; the desk card always shows the trip's time.
- Server + client. Migration 0476, switch `near_choice_enabled`. Size M.
- Player effect: a decision every trip, and loads that are not 96 % empty (play-test 3 #7, #8, #13).
- Load: a little more work in the card read; measure the beat before lighting. Depends on: 1.

**5. The clear-out: one place for each thing.** *Remove.* All four (each has one obvious home per act).
- What: the removal list X1-X8 (section 4). The bar becomes HQ · Trade · Fleets · Map · Land · Menu.
- Client only. Size M (many small edits; the navigation and look specs must be updated).
- Player effect: fewer buttons, one trade screen, nothing on screen that does not work yet.
- Depends on: 4 (X3's line replaces Routes).

### Retention: the first days (slices 6-9)

**6. The second hull earns while you are away.** *Change.* Melvor (automation unlock), Port Royale (convoys).
- What: at launch, "Own fleet: repeat your best route" or "Join Gaivota". The first sets a standing route at once (the route system is
  lit). The red crew line says "Hire 8 at the Inn" with the press; a met requirement reads "Trading 7 ✓". The While away card shows each
  route's earnings.
- Client only. Size S-M. Depends on: 2.
- Player effect: the moment income doubles, and a reason to come back to collect it (play-test 3 #26).

**7. Something can go wrong, a little, early.** *Add.* EVE (risk and insurance), Pirates! (a sail on the horizon).
- What: between Trading 3 and Master, once, a weak named pirate sights her on a near trip. Run, Pay or Fight with the real fight; the worst
  case is a small loss of cargo, never the ship; the arrival card explains what happened and what insurance and guns would have done. The
  Buy & sell sheet gains one line "If attacked: Flee" with the stance press, and insurance as a tick box.
- Server + client. Migration 0477, switch `early_sighting_enabled`. Size M.
- Load: about one fight per new company, ever. Depends on: 1.
- Player effect: the rule "something can go wrong" fires in hour one (play-test 3 #12, #25).

**8. First voyages: a short starter chain.** *Add.* Uncharted Waters (guild jobs), RS3 (the tutorial quest).
- What: five hand-placed jobs from her capital, built on hold-job rows, not a new system: (a) deliver near, (b) fetch from another harbour
  and deliver, (c) fill a hold with two goods, (d) survive the sighting of slice 7, (e) order the second ship. Each pays coin, Reputation
  and a named reward (a captain, a fitting, a flag). The goal card shows the chain's step.
- Server + client. Migration 0478, switch `first_voyages_enabled`. Size M.
- Owner row 311 ("no quests yet, add more stuff first"): decided by default that the stuff now exists (jobs, events, bounties, the yard),
  and these are jobs, not a story. The owner may reverse.
- Load: five rows per new company. Depends on: 3, 7.

**9. The logbook: discovery and collections.** *Add.* Uncharted Waters (port discovery), RS3 (collection log).
- What: a page of counts: harbours 3 / 254 by region, goods sold 4 / 629 by kind, seas 1 / 51, hulls, captains. A first arrival gets a
  "Discovered" moment with the port's specialty. Each finished set pays Reputation once and a small lasting benefit. The arrival card gains
  "New: Cinnamon · Spice 3 / 40".
- Server + client. Migration 0479, switch `logbook_enabled`. Size M (content plan item 6).
- Load: a row the first time only, about 1,200 rows in a company's life. Depends on: nothing.

### Depth: the first weeks (slices 10-14)

**10. One Skills page, with loud level-ups.** *Change.* RS3 (skills page), Melvor (xp an hour).
- What: the 11 sea levels and the land skills on one page, each tile with its level, the bar to the next, what it opens next, and "fastest
  way" (one press to the act that raises it). A level-up is a full card with what it opened. The Academy's 107 skills appear as captain
  studies from Trading 5 (X6).
- Client only. Size M. Depends on: 5.
- Built (decided by default, the owner may reverse each): the page is `/levels` ("Levels" / "레벨": "Skills" is already the
  Academy's word in docs/WORDS.md), lit under You (navTabs `under`, not a Menu row, so the Menu still holds its rows whole at
  320 x 640). You's levels card became its door: every level as its mark in a ring over its level, one press to the page. The page's
  MAIN is slice 9's ringed tiles, each now with what the next level that opens something opens (the chevron, the level, the served
  phrase: "3 +1 fleet") and at its foot FASTEST WAY: one link to the place of the level's first served activity that has a place
  (`live/levelWays.ts`, one table: trade, requests and route laps the Trade desk; ports, miles, supplies and rough water the Map;
  hulls and repairs the yard; investing Invest; crafting the Workstation; producing Production; digging and smithing Land). A place
  the level gate holds offers no press; a level not counted (Combat) and acts with no place (a storm, a fight's result) have none. The
  units differ, so the client never ranks activities: "first served" is the rule. SIDE: the Academy's 107 skills as STUDIES from
  Trading 5 (X6, the one level gate): held, a dimmed card whose figure is "Trading 5"; open, what is training now with its clock
  and each group as a ringed tile (studied of its skills), a press opening the Academy; then seamanship and the countries, moved with
  the tiles. THE LEVEL-UP CARD (`src/app/LevelUpCard.tsx`): a LEVEL_UP event new since the page's first read springs a full card over
  the glass's middle (the mark in an 88-px gold ring, slice 9's pop and rising chevron, the level large, a slow gold sunburst behind),
  what every level crossed opened as chips (`levelOpens`) and the Reputation it gave; it takes no press (pointer-events none, so it
  never stands between a player and a button) and goes by itself at 6 s. The arrival card keeps its level line and gift; its
  "Opens:" line moved to the card.

**11. Goods mastery.** *Add.* Melvor (mastery per item).
- What: each good a company sells has a small ladder (1-10): a slightly better sale price, a bigger daily cap, and at the top the good's
  name in gold on her cards. A trader gets a specialty.
- Server + client. Migration 0480, switch `goods_mastery_enabled`. Size M.
- Load: one row per company per good sold (at most 629 a company). Depends on: 9 (same "firsts" table pattern).

**12. Weekly orders.** *Add.* RS3 (weekly challenges), EVE (daily opportunities), without streaks.
- What: three orders a week per company, drawn from what the game already records ("sell 2,000 of spices", "finish a hold job in the
  Baltic", "drive off a pirate"). Each pays Reputation and a piece toward a title. Missing a week costs nothing (content plan 5.5: no
  streaks, no energy).
- Server + client. Migration 0481, switch `weekly_orders_enabled`. Size M.
- Load: three rows per company per week, checked at the existing settle points (no new minute job). Depends on: 10.

**13. Land feeds the sea.** *Change + light.* Melvor (skill chains), EVE (industry).
- What: Smithing is lit (`land_smith_enabled`, built); bars and nails made ashore count as yard materials in her Storage, and the yard sheet
  says "Make at home" beside each missing material. The Workstation returns (X6 reversed) once it has this use.
- Server (small, the yard reads Storage) + light the existing switch. Migration 0482, switch `land_feeds_yard_enabled`. Size M.
- Load: none per minute (settles on Collect). Depends on: 2, 10.

**14. Contracts between companies and houses.** *Add.* EVE (courier and item contracts).
- What: a merchant house, a city or a company posts a courier contract: carry these goods from A to B by a time, with collateral. The
  carrier risks the collateral (pirates, delay); the poster risks the goods. The board sits beside the hold jobs. The "Place a request"
  verb that nobody used becomes "Post a contract".
- Server + client. Migration 0483, switch `contracts_enabled`. Size L.
- Load: a cap on open contracts per house (start 1 per 20 houses, about 45 open). Depends on: 3, 7.

### The long game (slices 15-18)

**15. Nations matter.** *Change.* Uncharted Waters (nations), EVE (territory).
- What: the nation picked at sign-up gives a home perk (cheaper dock fees at its ports), nation jobs, and a weekly influence race shown on
  the map (cities tinted by who leads). The top contributors of the winning nation get Reputation and a title. Influence already exists;
  this makes it visible and paid.
- Server + client. Migration 0484, switch `nation_race_enabled`. Size M. Depends on: 12.

**16. Choices at sea.** *Add.* Uncharted Waters (events at sea), Pirates!.
- What: a storm, a wreck or a strange sail on a long trip: a choice, with a safe default for a player who is away. It touches the voyage's
  exact settle, so it goes last among the sea changes.
- Server + client. Migration 0485, switch `sea_choices_enabled`. Size L. Depends on: 7.

**17. A fleet that grows to five ships.** *Change.* Uncharted Waters (fleet growth), Port Royale.
- What: the third to fifth ships each open a role (a guard ship that lowers raid odds, a fast ship for hold jobs, a big hold for the
  route), and the goal card walks her up the ladder (ships, routes, captains). Uses room, armadas and convoys that exist.
- Server (roles as hull tags) + client. Migration 0486, switch `ship_roles_enabled`. Size M. Depends on: 6.

**18. Story lines.** *Add.* RS3 (quests with story).
- What: one short story per nation (four or five chapters) built on jobs, events and named pirates, ending in a special hull or captain.
  Only after slices 8 and 12 show players finish chains.
- Server + client. Switch `story_enabled`. Size L. Owner-gated (row 311). Depends on: 8, 12, 15.

## 7. How we will know

The play-test of section 4 in the playable doc, extended: a new player plays an hour and a day.
- **Hour one:** the card, sheet, arrival and purse agree on every trip; she finishes a job by one press; she chooses between two
  harbours on most trips; she is sighted once and understands what happened; she orders the second ship between minute 20 and 50 and
  still has a working purse.
- **Day one:** the second hull has run a route while she was away and the While away card says what it earned; the logbook shows at least
  one finished set; she has at least one weekly order half done.
- **On prod (read-only, one reader at a time):** new companies that make a second trip, order a second ship, and come back the next day.
