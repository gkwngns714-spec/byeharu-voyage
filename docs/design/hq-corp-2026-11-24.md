## A. The game in ten lines

1. You are a trading **company** with a **home city**, the city you were founded in. Your **HQ** is there, and so are your
   **warehouse** and your **estates**. You hold a **Rank**.
2. You never sail a ship by hand to earn money. You **hire people** (captains, and crew for them) and **assign them work** at HQ.
3. There are three kinds of work:
   - **sea trade**: a fleet buys a named good at A, then sells it at B or brings it home to the warehouse;
   - **land work**: a party digs or makes raw goods somewhere on land;
   - **estate work**: a mine or farm at HQ produces goods for the company.
4. Every assignment shows **+/- coin per hour** before you press Assign, and again while it runs. The **Ledger** adds every line, plus
   and minus, into one **net per hour**: income, wages, maintenance, tax, dock fees, stores, repairs and dividends.
5. **The decision** is choosing between real alternatives with the same people:
   - this captain on the Lisbon-Setubal iron run at +4,100/h, or in the home mine at +1,300/h;
   - sell the cork at Bristol for coin now, or bring it home for the yard;
   - a third ship, a second party, or an estate.

   The best option changes as prices, glut, levels and upkeep move. Finding it is the skill (EVE's market eye, Melvor's idle loop).
6. **First 10 minutes:** your HQ shows one fleet idle and one captain. Assign "Iron · Lisbon → Setubal · Sell", see +X/h, and press.
   Then assign the land party to Iron at home. Watch the Ledger turn green.
7. **First hour:** the fleet runs by itself. Trade levels rise and the first Rank step opens a second captain slot. You hire a captain at
   the Inn, compare two trades, and re-assign. You see the first maintenance line and the dividend from a small share.
8. **First week:** three fleets and two parties. A home mine estate is staffed by crew. Gold dug at Mining 60 is sold or kept.
   Higher Rank opens farther seas and lands, and cuts tax. Skills cut upkeep. You re-plan twice a day from the Ledger's per-hour lines.
9. **Risk is real but never strands you.** Pirates, glut and upkeep can make a line red. The home region and enough paying work are
   always open at Rank 1.
10. **The Map is where you look; HQ is where you act.** No other screen commits work.

## B. The model: one authority each

### B1. Worker: Captain, Crew

**Exists:**
- `public.officers` and `player_officers`, with `captain_state`, grades C/B/A/S, traits, captain skills (Reputation) and promotion.
  - Hire: `cmd.hire_officer`, which pays OFFICER_WAGE once, to the city.
  - Seat: `cmd.seat_captain` / `cmd.post_officer`.
- Crew: a count per hull (`ships.crew`), hired at an Inn with `cmd.do_hire` (`hire_crew_rate` per head) and let go with `cmd.do_dismiss`.
  - Crew wages accrue per sea-day (`wage_per_crew_day` x `running_cost_level`, `fleet_wages.owed`, at most 30 days).
  - They are charged only at a sale: `public.wages_due`, from the profit, never on a loss.

**Becomes:**
- A **Worker** is a captain, with the crew under her.
- A captain is **seated in a fleet** (as today) or **leads a land party** or **runs an estate** (new). One seat at a time; the seat is
  the assignment's.
- Crew stay a count on a hull for ships. For a party or an estate, crew is a count on that party or estate row (new, B4).

**Renamed (client):**
- The HQ area that shows them is **People** ("People" / "인력").
- Hiring stays at the Inn, which opens in place on HQ (S1).

**New:**
- **Captain slots**, opened by level (B5).
- **Wages ashore** for party and estate crew, through the same wage reader (`public.crew_wages`). Ashore there is no sale to take
  them from, so they are charged hourly (B4).

**Deleted:** nothing.

### B2. Asset: Fleet, Warehouse, Estate

**Fleet. Exists:**
- `fleets` / `ships`, with roles (0485), fleet room (0354; `fleet_hard_max` 6), and `cmd.move_ship` / `rename_fleet`.
- State is read only through `public.fleet_state_of` (CLAUDE.md).

Unchanged. Fleets stay a tab: the owner kept it in S7.

**Warehouse. Exists:**
- `public.player_storage(player, port, good)`, one shed per harbour, with capacity `storage_cap(port)` = the city's warehouse tier x 150 t.
- Doors: `STORE` / `TAKE` (0070), the forge (`cmd.forge_good`, 0281/0461), and Land output (`storage_put` at the party's home).

**Becomes:**
- **The Warehouse is the shed at the home city.** HQ shows it as one bar (used / cap) and its goods.
- Sheds elsewhere stay, as rows "Stored elsewhere".
- Goods still move only by ship.
- The NO-HOME law of `docs/FLEETS_AND_SLOTS.md` §1 ("there is no home base") is superseded by the owner's 2026-11-24 order: the
  company has a home. The physics stays: nothing moves between harbours without a ship. `public.home_port_of(player)` (0288, the
  founding port) is the one reading of "home".

**Estate. New, but not a new production engine:**
- An **Estate** is a building at HQ, such as a Mine or (later) a Farm, that runs **one land job of the home region forever**.
- Its rules are the Land frame's own rules:
  - the job: `land_actions`;
  - the site: `land_sites`, the home region's ground;
  - the settle: `land_settle`;
  - the haul: `storage_put` into the home Warehouse;
  - the market guard: 0457.
- What makes it an estate rather than a party:
  - it never marches, so a turn is the job's seconds alone;
  - it is staffed by a captain + crew;
  - it has a tier (bought) that sets its crew seats;
  - it pays upkeep.
- **Farm** needs the Farming skill rows: the `fields` ground and its `land_actions`, a data slice, as the land doc §5.1 planned.
  Until those rows exist only the **Mine** is offered.
- **Gold mine** = the Mine on the `gold` / `gold-dust` jobs (Mining 60), where the home region holds them.

The rules that tie them together:
- **The concept is one, the records are per worker.** A fleet holds at most one assignment (its `fleet_jobs` row). A party or an estate
  holds at most one job. HQ reads them through **one read**, `world.assignments()` (in 0495's rate read, below). It returns each
  assignment's worker, place, good, end, state, runs and **per hour**.
- **One door per kind.** Sea work goes through `cmd.set_fleet_job`; land and estate work through `cmd.land_send`. The area gate (B5)
  sits inside those doors.

### B4. Ledger: income and every cost line, as a RATE

**Exists:**
- `public.ledger(player, kind, ducats_delta, fleet_id, created_at)`. The only money writer is `public.credit`; `pay_city` and
  `city_pays` are the city legs. The closed economy's `mint_guard` refuses new coin.

**Cost kinds that exist today:**

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| TAX | Profit tax `profit_tax_at`: `tax_rate_default` 3 %, at most 20 %, x the country stance (home 0.25, ally 0.5, rival/war 2) |
| PORT_FEE | Dock fee: base + 0.5 % of the cargo, x country / regard / logbook / home -50 % |
| WAGES | Per sea-day, charged at the sale |
| PROVISION | Stores |
| REPAIR | Repairs |
| HIRE / OFFICER_WAGE | Hiring crew / a captain's signing fee |
| BUILD | The yard |
| INSURANCE | Insurance |
| LAND | Picks and mules |
| SHARE / DONATION | Investing and gifts |

There is **no maintenance, no storage fee, no building upkeep, no loan**. Today every cost is per event.

**The rate exists in one place:** `world.hq_board().rate`. It is `{per_hour: round(sum(ledger, last 24 h) / 24), net_24h}`, with
FOUNDING and probes left out (0480). The bridge shows it as "+X / hour" when the board is served. When the board is dark, v1 divides
today's Report by the hours since 00:00 (`useBoard.ts:240`): a second estimate, which S6 deletes.

**Becomes:**
- **ONE rate authority**, a server read `world.company_rate()` (0495). It uses the same rows and the same window as today's
  `hq_board.rate`, and `hq_board.rate` is re-pointed to call it. It returns:
  - `net_per_hour`;
  - `lines`: per hour by kind, grouped into plain lines: Trade, Land, Estates, Dividends, Wages, Maintenance, Tax, Dock, Stores,
    Repairs, Other;
  - `by_assignment`: per hour per fleet, from `ledger.fleet_id`, and per party or estate, from its own kinds;
  - `saved`: per hour that skills and Rank saved (B5).
- The client draws these figures. It never divides.
- **Before the press,** an assignment shows the server's quote:
  - sea: `world.good_board(good, fleet, from, qty)`, the desk's own read: profit and seconds per sale harbour, so profit / seconds as
    per hour;
  - land: `world.land`'s served terms: units per trip and trip seconds, times the price.

  That is the server's estimate, not a second client rule. Upkeep and wages per hour are added from the same cost functions.
- **New cost lines** (section D sizes them, all dark):
  - **MAINTENANCE**: hourly upkeep of each hull and each estate, `public.upkeep_due`, paid to the home city by `pay_city`. Money is
    moved, not destroyed.
  - **WAGES ashore**: party and estate crew, hourly.
- **Dividends hourly** (0494): see D6.

**Deleted:** the client's v1 rate (S6). Stats > Me's money block is already gone (S8).

### B5. Rank and levels: one ladder, three levers

**Exists:**

| Piece | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Title** | `public.title_level` = the lower of Trading and Exploration, 11 names in `title_names`. Before 2026-11-23 it gated seas through `sea_refusal` while `sea_conditions_bite` was on; the owner turned that off because he "can't send ships because of restriction". |
| **Levels** | 11 sea levels, 1-20: Trading, Exploration and Combat counted from the record; Supplies, Weather, Shipbuilding, Requests, Craft, Routes, Investment and Evasion granted. Land skills run 1-100 on `level_curves`: Mining, and Smithing (dark). |
| **`level_perks`** (0417, re-cut 0453 and 0468) | **The one table of "what level N gives"**: `(perk, level 2-20, skill, unit pct_less / pct_more / count, amount)`, read only by `public.level_perk`. Today it holds `supplies_price`, `repair_price`, `gear_wear`, `storm_wear` (cost cuts), `route_room` and 0468's Trading unlocks (routes, ships per fleet, yard fee, fleet room). Behind `level_perks_on` / `level_unlocks_enabled`. |
| **The client gate** | `live/levelGate.ts` (X6): Academy, Workstation and Invest from Trading 5. |
| **Reputation, Influence, the country race (0484)** | Reputation buys captain skills. Influence is per city; the mayor is the top investor. The race's Champion is a weekly title. |

**Becomes:**
- **Rank = the Title**, `public.title_level`, the one reader. HQ's Rank strip shows:
  - the rank's name and a bar to the next;
  - what the next step opens, from `level_perks`;
  - your place in your home city, from the Influence ranking of that city.

  OWNER'S: decided by default. The owner said "in your city, you have a rank". The title is the ladder that exists; the city place
  is the city half of it, read from Influence. Neither is new.
- **Lever 1, levels gate AREAS** (0500):
  - Every sea harbour and land region gets a **tier**, computed from data: its sailing hours from the company's home
    (`sea_reach_matrix`, 0482). It is NOT a hand list in code.
  - A tier needs a Rank. Land jobs also keep their own skill level (`land_actions.level`, as today).
  - It is enforced in **one server function** `public.area_refusal(player, place)`, called by the assignment doors (`set_fleet_job`,
    `fleet_job_plan`'s pick, `land_send`) and by the hand sail. **`sea_refusal`'s title rule is replaced by it.**
  - `sea_conditions_bite` stays the owner's knob and is not flipped.
  - On screen it is a lock with "Rank N", on the Map and in Assign, never a silent refusal.
  - **Never strands a player:** tier 1 (the home region and everything within 1,440 sailed miles, half a Barca hour; economy file
    section 5) is open at Rank 1, and the first locked area names what opens it.
- **Lever 2, levels UNLOCK capacity** (0497): new rows in **`level_perks`**, the same table and the same reader (economy file
  section 4):
  - `captain_slots` (count, on Rank)
  - `party_slots` (count, on Mining)
  - `estate_slots`, `estate_tier`, `area_tier`, `shed_room` (count, on Rank)
  - (no `hull_tier`: Academy skills already open hull classes, docs/LEVELS.md section 1)

  They stand beside 0468's fleets, ships per fleet and routes (routes become "assignment slots" once routes are deleted).
  **No second unlock system.** `levelGate.ts`'s client table reads the served perk instead of its own constant.
  - Each new cap starts at the most any company holds on prod at deploy, so no one loses a captain or a party (read in the slice).
- **Lever 3, skills cut MAINTENANCE; Rank cuts TAX** (0496): rows on the existing cost functions:
  - `upkeep` (`pct_less`) on Shipbuilding, beside today's `repair_price`. `public.upkeep_due` reads `level_perk(player, 'upkeep')`.
  - `tax_rank` (`pct_less`) and `dock_rank` (`pct_less`) keyed on the Title. `profit_tax_at` / the dock fee multiply by one more
    factor, `public.rank_factor(player, kind)`.
  - **Floors:** no cut takes a cost under 50 % of its base.
  - The Ledger shows **"Saved +X / h"** from `company_rate().saved`.
  - Because `level_perks` is keyed by `skill`, the Title needs a `level_skills` row `rank` that reads `title_level`. If that cannot be
    a plain row, the builder adds one branch to `level_perk`, not a second table.

## D. Economy and balance: the owner's questions, with my defaults

**How money works today** (read from the chain):
- The economy is closed. `economy_closed` and the city purses are lit on prod. `public.mint_guard` refuses every "minted" kind at
  `credit` and `treasury_post`.
- **Coin enters a company** only from a city purse (`city_pays`): sales, requests, bounties, dividends, insurance, contracts, the
  capital's gift and founding.
- **Coin leaves to a city** (`pay_city`): buys, tax, dock fees, wages, stores, repairs, hiring, building, insurance, shares, gifts and
  land gear.
- Only `SLOT` (fleet room) is destroyed.

So nothing below mints coin. Every new cost is moved to a city purse.

**The existing knobs are the owner's and are not changed by this plan:**
`economy_closed`, `city_treasury_enabled`, `city_reserve_enabled`, `tax_rate_default` (0.03), `tax_rate_max` (0.20), `port_fee_base`,
`port_fee_pct`, `running_cost_level` (0.2), `wage_per_crew_day`, `hire_crew_rate`, `works_coupon_day` (0.018 on prod), `warehouse_tuns_per_tier`
