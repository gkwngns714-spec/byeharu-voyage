# The remaster (2026-11-24): the whole game becomes the HQ company

**The owner:** *"you need to rework on the game, remaster it. i am changing the whole game right?"* Yes. This file is the one order of
work; details stay in `hq-corp-2026-11-24.md`, its economy file and `civ-layer-2026-11-24.md`. **OWNER'S: decided by default, reversible.**

## The rule
A screen or rule that is not part of the HQ company is replaced and DELETED, not patched. Real time; companies, coin, ships and levels
are kept (no wipe); no PvP or conquest.

| Keep (the world underneath) | Rebuild (what the player sees and does) | Delete when its replacement is live |
|---|---|---|
| map, harbours, markets, merchants, pirates | HQ: workers, Assign, Ledger, Rank, Warehouse, buildings | Trade, Office and Land tabs |
| closed money, city purses, countries | one action shape for sea, land, estate, making | standing routes; Best / Land / Repeat chips |
| ships, captains, levels, Reputation | policies, crew slots, country works | v1 board ranking and rate |
| Map and Fleets as places to look | the per-hour figure and its reasons | raw error codes on any screen |

## One travel rule (new, from the owner's E_LEG_TOO_FAR report)
Measured on prod, 2026-11-24: a `land` job at Island of Mozambique planned one 7,052 nm leg to LIS against `nav_leg_nm_base` 1,700 and stopped
with E_LEG_TOO_FAR (its params say CAD; why the plan says LIS is not yet known). HQ printed the code (`JobsAway.tsx`: `j.paused`).
- A job's trip is planned **through harbours**, each leg one she can sail. If no chain exists, the job is not offered.
- Assign never offers work the server would refuse. A stopped job shows a plain reason and one press that fixes it.
- Row 369 removed sea limits, yet the range a leg is still one. Through-harbour planning makes it unseen; the knob is not touched.
