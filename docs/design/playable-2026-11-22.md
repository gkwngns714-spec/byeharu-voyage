## 1. The play-test (one hour, local build, phone 390x844 Korean and desktop 1280x800 English)

Six real trips; no crash, console clean. Screenshots were taken locally, not kept.

**What is good, and stays:** the arrival card ("Arrived at Setubal · Sold 20 Quicksilver · Profit +4,399 · New port · Trading level 2") is the one moment that feels like a reward. First trips take 60-75 s. The dark look, flags and large numbers read well. The desktop Trade desk (cities, goods, ship) reads at a glance. The map and its port panel. The cargo page that lists every place to sell. Progress continues with the tab closed.

## 2. What comparable games do

| Game | What makes it a game | What this game has of it |
|---|---|---|
| **Uncharted Waters Online / Origin** (the reference) | A nation and a home port picked at the start; a first-hour story that teaches buy, sail, sell, the yard; trade, adventure and battle fame; ports that ask for goods. | The world, the fame ladder and port investment. No start choice, no first hour. |
| **Sid Meier's Pirates!** | Every voyage can turn: a sail on the horizon, chase or flee, a town that changes hands. Short sessions, constant small decisions. | The fight, Run and Pay are built; pirates never touch a company (slice A fixes). |
| **Port Royale 4 / Patrician IV** | The player reads prices and chooses where to go; convoys on auto-routes earn while the player builds; buildings and supply chains; the town grows from what you bring. | Auto-routes (live), workshops/forge (unused), city development (built, little used). The choice of where to go is made for the player. |
| **Taipan! / Merchant RPGs** | Simple loop, but with risk (pirates, storms, a loan shark), a debt to clear and a clear goal number. | No risk, no goal number. |
| **Offworld Trading Company / EVE trading** | Other traders move the same market; your sale moves the price; information is the edge. | Markets drift and react; the 845 merchant houses are invisible as rivals. |
| **Idle/async (Melvor Idle, OGame, Travian)** | Timers that end while you are away; a "while you were gone" summary; something to spend on every return; an always-visible next goal. | Land skills and routes run away; While away card live. Spending on return is missing (slice C). |

**Four rules taken from them:**
1. **A choice every trip.** The player picks between at least two different answers (near and safe vs far and rich), never one pre-chosen answer.
2. **Something can go wrong**, and the player can prepare for it (guns, escorts, insurance, routes through calmer seas).
3. **Money always has the next thing to buy**, shown on screen with its price and wait.
4. **The next goal is always on screen, in plain words**, with its reward.

## 3. The plan, in order

**P0, now: a new player can read the game (client only, two builders).**
- **First-run:** a short welcome card on the first ready read: who you are (a trading house in Lisbon, 1507), the goal ("earn your second ship"), and the first press ("pick a good, press Buy"). Shown once.
- **Labels:** the two card prices get their words (Buy / Sell, or Here / There); the goal card says what 0/4 counts and opens the step that is next.
- **The buy default** leaves money for the next trip: the sheet opens at the hold or half the coin, whichever is smaller (decided by default); the profit estimate stands above the fold.
- **Builder notes off the player's screens** ("counted only", "conditions off", "not counted yet", the raw Room line).
- **One number per thing:** the title, ships n/cap, crew, recent trades and the sale toast read the one source each.
- **Korean words:** no English good, place or sea name inside a Korean sentence; spacing in ledger lines.
- **Small:** toasts clear of the header; the sailing card's money line not in red; Next on the arrival card goes somewhere Close does not; a cheap price is not red.

**P1, this week: the loop gets its missing halves (migrations, dark until the owner lights them).**
- **A. Pirates raid companies too** (0464, PR #777, dark behind `raid_companies_enabled`): a company under Master is passed over.
- **C. The yard takes an order** (0465, in build, dark): coin and a real wait buy a ship.
- **A choice of where to sell:** the buy sheet shows the best three places to sell (time, profit), and the player picks one; the best one stays the default.
- **Requests a ship can carry** (content plan item 4): each harbour posts hold-sized jobs with a deadline in hours.

**P2, after:** the houses' board (rank among the 845 merchant houses, by week); each level opens something real; named world events (famine, blockade, festival) on the fair's rails; bounties on named pirates.

**Not doing** (unchanged from the content plan 5.5): quests and story chains, login streaks and energy, PvP, more goods, ports or hulls.

## 4. How we will know

The plain test: a new player plays for an hour, always knows what to do next, chooses where to sell, is raided once by the time they are Master, and orders a second ship before the hour is out.
